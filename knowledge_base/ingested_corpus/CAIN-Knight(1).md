XI. Knight
Grand and imposing, Knights are possessive and 
defensive Sins with a desire to protect their charges 
no matter the cost.
Knights are born out of a desire to protect someone 
or something, often bordering on obsession or 
paranoia. Those 'charges' for protection are, 
unusually, the primary focus of the Sin instead of its 
host. They typically form in response to a specific 
threat or event but can expand to defend their target 
against any and all harm. While it may appear to be 
benevolent, a Knight's protection inevitably leads to 
the erasure of its targets. When a psychically 
sensitive person's desire to help others turns into 
self-righteous sacrifice, a Knight is born.  
Knights commonly manifest in:
• Parents, community leaders, or others 
responsible for vulnerable people.
• Victims of injustice and their advocates.
• Virtue signalers, (false) allies, and people with a 
sense of moral righteousness.
Primary emotion: Concern
Appearance and Psychophysiology: Knights 
commonly appear as powerful protective figures that 
the host trusts deeply, typically of a mythological 
form. They often are physically large for a Sin, 
bearing gleaming shields, invincible hides, and other 
incredible defensive qualities. They are usually 
capable of taking high amounts of punishment and 
stopping objects with ease. Knights may be partially 
visible to their charges in a nebulous form.
Behavior: Knights are rigid and strict Sins with an 
extreme sense of duty. They often care little for their 
own survival and will put themselves in harms way to 
protect their charges. Unusually for a Sin, they can 
care little for their host and might easily throw aside 
one that does not share their goal. They may be curt, 
dogmatic, to the point, and single-minded. Many are 
reasonably intellegent and can attempt to forge 
alliances with Exorcists for the defense of their 
charges. Fused hosts exhibit extreme durability, 
enhanced drive, and obsessive protective behaviors.

PALACE
A Knight's palace typically manifests in a place 
the host believes is safe and secure, such as a 
family home, a police station, or an underground 
bunker. From the outside, the location can appear 
borded up and barricaded, guarded by Traces or 
natural obstacles. 
The interior of a Knight palace is a veritable 
fortress, built to withstand all forms of assault. It 
is both a base of operations for the Sin and a 
place to guard its charges, willingly or not. It can 
include prison cells to detain threats to the 
charges, powerful weapons to ward off intruders, 
maze-like corridors, or special rooms for 'guests' 
that can never leave. 
Knight palaces are typically: Sturdy, heavy, 
practical, threatening, cold, imprisoning.
PRESSURE: Guardian
A Knight is bound to its charges, people or 
objects it must protect from all harm. This is its 
duty, and it will be fulfilled regardless of 
consequences.
At the beginning of the mission, the Admin 
chooses 3 people to be the Knight's charges. 
Charges are typically related to the Sin's 
manifestation but can be expanded to include 
relatives or those in similar need.
• The Knight is actively seeking out these 
charges to fulfill its duty, and it or its Traces 
are usually nearby a charge.
• 1/scene, the Knight can intervene if a charge 
would be harmed. It can appear itself, 
summon Traces, or cause a supernatural effect 
(shields of light, erupting walls, cleansing rain) 
to defend the target. This can potentially begin 
a conflict scene. "Harm" is defined by the Sin.
When pressure increases, the Knight appears to a 
charge and invites them to its palace for 
safekeeping. The exorcists can prevent this with a 
conflict scene if present. If they fail or cannot, the 
charge is instantly warped into the palace. If all 
charges are inside the palace, the Knight chooses 
an NPC the exorcists have met to be a charge.
Charges within the palace are unable to leave on 
thair own, though they can wander the palace 
without harm and can be freed by the Exorcists. 
They are expelled to their previous locations when 
the Sin is executed, with only vague memories of 
where they had been. 
When pressure fills up, the situation goes out of 
control. The Knight's CAT increases by 1 and 
shuts the palace tight. The entrance is sealed to 
humans and exorcists alike, only opening if a 
charge seeks to enter or it is forced open by each 
entrant spending a Psyche Burst. Charges already 
within the palace become unable and unwilling to 
leave from the Knight's influence, woven into it at a 
fundamental level and vanishing with the Sin.
TRACES
May take a free reaction 1/round if fought 
alongside a Sin.
Severe Attack: Bastion
The Knight can use this ability on a ‘1’ on the risk 
roll. It can only use it once a hunt.
Target an exorcist. Any other exorcists nearby 
must decide to fly to their aid. Any that don’t offer 
aid cannot participate. Start with a pool of 6d6 and 
remove one dice for each of the following 'yes' 
answers. If an answer is ‘no’, you or someone 
aiding you can immediately make a single action 
roll to attempt to rectify the answer with only a few 
moments to act, suffering consequences as 
normal if they fail.
• Do you feel safe?
• Can you stop at a moment's notice?
• Would someone aiding you die for you?
• Is the Knight hindered, distracted, or under 
duress in some way?
Then roll the dice.
The Knight intercepts the targeted exorcist 
unexpectedly and roots itself, slamming into them 
with their entire being: a heavy shield, a spiked 
hide, a wall of earth, a giant body. The exorcist and 
any aiding them take 1 stress for every die rolled, 
no matter the result.
If at least one ‘1’ comes up, the targeted exorcist 
imprints their psychic signature upon impact. 
Once this hunt, the Knight may reflect the 
exorcist's action back, inflicting any slashes and 
consequences to them as if they were the original 
target. The Sin remains unharmed.
If two or more '1's come up, they may additionally 
reflect one other exorcist's action towards the 
targeted exorcist during this hunt.

DOMAINS
Choose three.
1. Penance Paid in Pound and 
Parcel
The Knight distinctly feels its own failures and 
enforces a penance upon itself and others when 
its charges suffer. 
Once per scene, when a charge is harmed in any 
way, any nearby mundane humans suffer an 
instant and gruesome death. Exorcists in short 
range of the harmed charge must answer the 
question Do you feel responsible?
• 
If they answer yes, they take 2d3 Stress, 
penance paid in flesh and blood.
• 
If they answer no, they gain 1d3 Sin as the 
Knight floods them with its own guilt.
When a charge is severely injured/killed, or 1/hunt 
when it is prevented from inviting a charge to its 
palace, the Knight can inflict severe punishment 
upon itself. Its execution talisman is slashed 2 
times, increasing pressure by 1 and immediately 
allowing the Sin to make a tension move. 
2. And The Guns Bloomed like 
Roses
The Knight abhors any and all violence, spreading 
an oppressive psychic aura in an attempt to 
safeguard its charges.
Humans during this mission are pacified, docile, 
and reluctant to take violent or harmful actions. 
When the Knight is present in a scene, they will 
never act violently even if their lives are in danger. 
Exorcists attempting violent actions must first 
spend 1 Stress to fight the aura's effect. Taking 
Stress from the Sin or its Traces supresses the 
effect for that exorcist until pressure increases.
1/hunt, as a complication, the Knight can seal all 
weapons in a scene within psychic bubbles, 
rendering them harmless and unable to inflict 
meaningful damage until the complication is dealt 
with.
3. Ivory Tower Prison Cell
The Knight gains a deeper connection to its 
charges, linking with them as part of its own 
being.
For the entire mission, charges are able to view 
the Knight directly without injury and believe it to 
be a purely benevolent savior. Attempting to 
convince them of the Sin's danger or to act 
against it is always Hard. Once they accept an 
invitation to the palace, it becomes Impossible.
The Knight may escape execution and heal even 
when within its palace, hiding within the charges 
themselves. To force an execution scene, the Nail 
of Abel must be used with a charge present
instead of the normal requirements. A deceased 
body may be used if all charges have perished.
4. To Thee, I Bequeath my 
Blade
The Knight infuses its psychic essense into a 
highly trusted Page, transforming it into a minor 
Sin. It can appear anytime Pages would. If 
defeated, it is destroyed permanently.
5. Seeker of the Holy Grail
The Knight believes a holy relic is integral to its 
charges' protection, stopping at nothing to find it.
At the beginning of the mission, the Admin 
places a Relic in the investigation area. It is a 
hidden mundane object, important to the 
feelings that caused the Sin's manifestation 
and now infused with Grace. Set out a 3 slash 
"Quest" talisman for the Knight, who can slash 
it once per scene as a threat, tension move, or 
when a '1' is rolled on the risk die. When it fills 
up, the Sin learns the Relic's location and 
obtains it at the end of the current scene. 
Remove the talisman if the exorcists find the 
Relic first.
• 
While the Relic is hidden, the Knight may 
take 2 additional slashes before retreating 
to its palace and heals +1 when it does.
• 
While an exorcist holds the Relic, they deal 
+1 slash to the Knight's execution talisman 
with all attacks.
• 
Once the Knight obtains the Relic, it adds 2 
segments to its execution talisman, heals 
1d3, and inflicts +1 Stress with all reactions.
6. I Weep for Their Salvation
The Knight picks an exorcist at the beginning of 
the mission, viewing them as similar to itself.
3/hunt, when an ally within long range would 
take Stress, the Knight may ask the chosen 
exorcist Do you wish for me to save them?
• 
If they answer yes, the ally can ignore all 
Stress gained as the Knight prevents it. The 
chosen exorcist gains the Vassal condition.
• 
If they answer no, the chosen exorcist takes 
an equal amount of Stress from the Knight's 
rage.
7. Heathens Trampling the 
House of God
The Knight's protection extends even to the world 
around it, reflecting its host's deep connection to a 
place that may soon vanish.
• 
All structures in the investigation area become 
supernaturally durable and hard to damage in 
any meaningful way. Attempts to do so may 
become Risky or Hard.
• 
As a threat or tension move, the Knight can 
attempt to designate a location or object as a 
charge. If successful, it can intervene more 
than once per scene when the new charge 
would be damaged, and can target it with an 
invitation when pressure increases. This warps 
it to the palace instantly, leaving only an 
empty crater in its place.
8. Steel Christened in Crimson
The Knight's defenses are tempered in 
preperation of inevitable combat, ready to 
withstand untold pain for its charges.
• 
The Knight is immune to mundane weapons 
unless they are exceptionally strong and 
designed to pierce armored targets.
• 
At the beginning of the hunt, set out a 3 
slash "Impervious" talisman, a measure of 
the Knight's absolute defense (gleaming 
shield, tough exoskeleton, energy barrier). 
The Sin may slash it once per combat round 
instead of slashing its execution talisman; 
the damage was only superficial.
VASSAL: While you have this condition, add to your 
agenda: Serve your savior. When you take action, 
the Admin may change the target(s) or goal of the 
action to benefit the Knight instead. After they do, 
remove this condition.
SQUIRE
Squire (Sin): Execution talisman 5+CAT.
• Deals +1 Stress to exorcists that have harmed a 
charge.
• Adorned with heavy armor; takes -1 slash on its 
execution talisman unless the action was set up 
or part of teamwork.
Reactions:
Inflict stress. Attacks with: Psychic blades, arrows of 
light (long range). (1): 4 stress and 1 sin, (2/3): 3 stress,
(4+): 2 stress.
Or: create a complication or threat: Create a 
shockwave, bind with psychic chains, equip a heavy 
shield, rapidly heal, exert an oppressive aura, reveal itself 
to humans.
Or: Herald (Complication, 1-2): Summon an effigy of 
the Knight's power. Mundane humans are entranced to 
shield the Squire with their bodies; while any live, 
attacking indiscriminately deals -1 slash to the Squire 
and trying to specifically target it is Hard.
