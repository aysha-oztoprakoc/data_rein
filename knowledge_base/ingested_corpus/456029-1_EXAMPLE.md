HEX WARRIOR: When attacking with a melee weapon you can use your Charisma  
modifier for the attack and damage rolls. 
 
HEXBLADE'S CURSE: Bonus Action, Range 30ft, 1 Creature, 1 Minute. You gain the 
following benefits:  
- Bonus damage equal to your proficiency bonus (+4). 
- Critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20. 
- If the cursed target dies, you regain hit points equal to your warlock level + your 
Charisma modifier (15).
PACT OF THE BLADE: You can use your action to create a pact 
weapon in your emply hand. This weapon counts as magical for the 
purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical 
attacks and damage. Your pact weapon disappears if it is more than 5 
feet away from you for 1 minute or more. It also disappears if you 
use this feature again. if you dismiss the weapon (no action 
required), or if you die.
When you slay a humanoid, you can cause its spirit to rise  
from its corpse as a specter. It gains temporary hit points equal 
to half your warlock level (6). Roll initiative for the specter, 
which has its own turns. It obeys your verbal commands, and it 
gains a bonus to its attack rolls equal to your Charisma modifier 
(+4). Lasts until you finish a long rest, recharges on long rest.
If the target cursed by your Hexblade's Curse  
hits you with an attack roll, you can use your 
reaction to roll a d6. On a 4 or higher, the 
attack instead misses you, regardless of its roll.
True Seeing
DEVIL'S SIGHT: You can see normally in darkness, both magical and  
nonmagical, to a distance of 120 feet. 
 
THIRSTING BLADE: You can attack with your pact weapon twice, instead of once, 
whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. 
 
ELDRITCH SMITE: Once per turn when you hit a creature with your pact weapon, 
you can expend a warlock spell slot to deal an extra 1d8 force damage to the 
target, plus another 1d8 per level of the spell slot, and you can knock the target 
prone if it is Huge or smaller. 
 
LIFEDRINKER: When you hit with your pact weapon, deal necrotic damage equal 
to Charisma modifier (+4) 
 
RELENTLESS HEX: As a bonus action, you can magically teleport up to 30 feet to 
an unoccupied space you can see within 5 feet of the target cursed by your hex 
spell or by a warlock feature of yours. To teleport in this way, you must be able 
to see the cursed target. 
 
ONE WITH SHADOWS: In dim light or darkness, use an action to turn invisible 
until you move or take an action or reaction.
