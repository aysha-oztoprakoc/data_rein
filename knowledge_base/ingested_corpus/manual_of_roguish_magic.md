elcome to the Manual of Roguish Magic – a quick collection of interesting magic items 
specifically created for rogues and thieves. Each of the ten items in this list confers 
abilities onto rogues that help them in their sneaky endeavors, whether around town or 
in the deepest, darkest dungeons.  
 
Each item requires attunement and can only be used by someone who is a thief.  
 
Next time you build out rewards, consider adding one of these items to the loot. It will surely make 
your rogue’s eyes light up with surprise and appreciation. 
 
So, what are you waiting for? 
 
Grab an item and make your favorite thief’s day. 
 
May all your adventures be spectacular!

1. Goggles of Deep Vision -  The goggles are made of black rubber with a rubber strap that keeps 
them snugly on the rogue’s face when worn. The two lenses are made of opaque black glass. 
When worn, they block all sight except for the object being examined. By concentrating, the 
rogue can use the properties of the goggles to peer inside that object to a depth determined 
by the rogue’s desire. Whether examining the intricacies of a lock layer by layer, a trap built 
into a door, or the construction of a chest, this device helps the rogue peel back the secrets 
beneath the object’s surface. (Note: The goggles cannot be used to peer beyond the object. 
For example, the rogue cannot see through a door into the room beyond or examine the 
contents of a chest before opening it.) When worn, the rogue is granted a +2 to any ability 
check related to opening locks, disabling traps, etc.  
 
2. Gloves of the Shadow Thief - These gloves are made of black leather and are decorated with 
intricate patterns of silver thread. They grant the wearer a +1 bonus to Dexterity checks and 
saving throws. Additionally, the wearer can use an action to cast the pass without trace spell 
on themselves once per day. This spell lasts for 1 hour or until the wearer ends it.  
 
3. Boots of the Cat - These boots are made of soft, black leather and are decorated with light tan 
and white feathers. They grant the wearer a +1 bonus to Dexterity checks and saving throws 
when climbing. Additionally, the wearer can use an action to cast the feather fall spell on 
themselves once per day. This spell lasts for 1 minute or until the wearer lands. 
 
4. Crown of Aluminum – This thin, pliable metal crown fits snugly in a leather cap, merging so 
completely with the leather that it is quite comfortable and crinkle-free. When worn, it 
protects the rogue from invasive psychic probes and mind-reading spells, preventing thoughts 
from being read and reducing psychic damage attacks by half.  
 
5. Heartfelt Dagger – This magical +1 dagger is made of obsidian, magically strengthened so it 
can’t break. The hilt has a small reservoir capable of holding enough poison for five doses and 
a thin channel carved into the dagger to deliver the poison on a successful hit. The dagger can 
hold any poison allowed by the game master, and each dose delivers poison damage based on 
the choice of poison. The target takes half damage from the poison on a DC 14 Constitution 
saving throw. The rogue can also command the dagger to inject multiple doses of poison up to 
the maximum held in the reservoir. Once the reservoir runs dry, it takes an action to be refilled. 
 
6. Psychic Scroll – This blank scroll can peer into the mind of the intended target and quickly fill 
itself with content appropriate to the task. Thus it can become official travel papers, an 
invitation to a high society ball, orders from the governor to impound a certain object or any 
other document that will help the rogue accomplish their mission. The scroll can be used three 
times daily. If the target rolls a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw, the scroll will not function, and 
they will see a blank piece of parchment. 
 
 
7. Cloak of Sidestteping – This luxurious fur-lined gray cloak allows the rogue to shift five feet in 
any direction as a reaction. Originally built for a rogue to avoid being struck by a poison dart 
from a failed attempt at disabling a trap, clever rogues discover its value in combat and other 


situations. 
 
8. Trumpet of Aural Perceptiveness – A gnarled, sea-stained piece of driftwood carved in the 
shape of a hearing trumpet can be inserted into a rogue’s ear, whereupon it collapses into the 
ear and disappears from view, becoming a permanent part of the rogue until pulled out. The 
trumpet gives the rogue a +2 to passive perception and any perception checks relating to all 
sounds within 50’, even if the sound is behind a thick wall.  
 
9. Roving Eye – This small oval object made of iron, gold, and mercury amalgam has been painted 
to resemble a humanoid eyeball. As an action, the rogue can toss it into the air and direct it to 
travel up to 30’ per round, up to a maximum of 100’. As long as the rogue concentrates on the 
roving eye, they can see through it with the same clarity of vision as though the rogue were 
actually in the eye’s position. A single action can be used to both move the eye and look 
through it. If the rogue chooses to use an action for some other activity, the eye will freeze at 
its current location, awaiting the rogue’s next command.  
 
10. The Key – The Key is a fabled skeleton key carved from bone. It can open any door, chest, or 
other locked object. It automatically arranges itself to match the lock’s key perfectly. It safely 
opens any object, effectively bypassing any trap that may be configured to be released from a 
failed lockpicking attempt. The Key can be used once per day.
