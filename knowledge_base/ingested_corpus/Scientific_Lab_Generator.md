# Scientific_Lab_Generator.pdf

 
 
Scientific Lab Generator 
There are many random dungeon generators and 
town or city generators, but I don’t think I have seen 
a mad scientist’s lab generator. It just felt right that a 
mad scientist should be using d12s, so here is a 
collection of d12 tables to help you describe a lab. 
Not every table will be needed in every situation. 
Pick and choose as needed.
