NIGHTS OF CHROME 
and Fan Material 
 
NIGHTS OF CHROME is a hack, a revision and an augmented manual based on 
CYBERPUNK VIEJA ESCUELA, and published by GRAPAS Y MAPAS. NIGHTS OF CHROME adapts, 
expands and reconfigures the original rules, being a generic manual for urban, futuristic or 
cyberpunk themed adventures. 
 
If as a fan you want to publish fan material based on NIGHTS OF CHROME, either in free 
or paid format, you can do it freely. You can create supplements, expansions or modules, and use 
the NIGHTS OF CHROME rules without restrictions. However, if you are going to refer to the 
original rules present in the NIGHTS OF CHROME manual, before adding them to the supplement, 
I would be grateful if you could refer to the original manual, taking advantage of the fact that it 
is completely free. If you think it is necessary to add a specific fragment to the new supplement 
for a better understanding, there is no problem. 
 
If on the other hand, the intention is to make a complete, specific and independent 
manual, based on NIGHTS OF CHROME (which in turn is based on CYBERPUNK VIEJA ESCUELA 
and other related supplements), you must respect the Creative Commons Attribution - 
NonCommercial - ShareAlike license terms.
