THE ISHMAEL 
PROTOCOL
The hunt for the Moby Dick was Captain Ahab’s obsession, attempting to find and 
get revenge on the natural world for what he had lost. Building a ragtag team of 
any that would join his bloodthirsty fight for revenge, in many ways, the average 
exorcist can find himself represented by his men. bloodied, bruised, found dead 
after it all ends.
You are sailing the sea. You have no option but to fight, so make it worth our 
while. Maybe you will survive, like the worthy Ishmael. Maybe you will be the one 
to kill the moby dick.
The Ishmael Protocol is to be issued only in times of extreme pressure on exorcist 
squads, such as the coming tides of war. The blasphemies and agendas herein 
represent severe threats for the self and those around you, and are meant as a 
last resort for massively outmatched exorcists.
The agendas, you may find also are meant for times of war. Those who fight with 
only tools they know will die on the battlefield. Exploring the unknown of what you 
can be is the only way for a psychic to survive in this dog eat dog world. Be it 
finding what you are through your agenda, or discovering what the eldritch power 
of the sea can offer you, through the blasphemies hidden here.
The only organization to ever rival CAIN in power and influence, UNDER // 
HEAVEN, was destroyed in the midst of the Cold war. Due to this, it had been 
growing increasingly unlikely that the Ishmael protocol would ever need to be 
issued again, until now.
The reemergence of Patience, The growing power of ABEL and many other 
organizations have made us at AUTHORITY weary. Therefore, we grant you these 
weapons to hunt those Under Heaven.
Choose your weapons well, Hunter. You don't want to end up like Ahab.
Hunt The white whale.
