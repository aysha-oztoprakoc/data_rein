# Magic_and_Cursed_Item_Pack.pdf



Magic and Cursed Item Pack
Weapons
Cursed Blade of Soul Devouring
Weapon (greatsword), legendary
This blade is cursed with a great power and a great evil. On
contact with a living creature, it extends out tendrils and
merges itself into the flesh of the creature, becoming
permanently attached to the dominant appendage unless
removed by a wish spell or similarly powerful magic. The
blade has a mind and will of its own, it will try to drive its
wielder to slay as many humanoids as possible to absorb
their souls. With each soul it absorbs, it gets a +1 to necrotic
damage, but the force on the mind grows stronger. After each
new soul that is absorbed, the creature must make a DC
15+Number of Souls Absorbed Wisdom saving throw, on
failure they suffer three levels of exhaustion. If this level of
exhaustion would cause them to die, they are instead taken
full control of by the sword, going on an endless rampage for
more souls until the sword is removed. When the sword is
removed, all previously gathered souls leave the sword, and
the person who was wielding it has five levels of exhaustion if
they were taken control of.
Spear of Bees
Weapon(spear), legendary (requires attunement)
This spear can be placed in the ground once per day,
summoning three Giant Bees, which have the same statblock
as the Giant Scorpion. These Bees are loyal to whoever
placed the spear in the ground, and will follow any command
to the death. At the end of the day, any remaining bees vanish
into a yellow light and go back into the spear.
Spear of Daylight
Weapon(spear), very rare (requires attunement)
This appears as a normal spear except for some small
markings on its side. When a creature attunes itself to it, it
assigns a command word to it. When the command word is
uttered, a sphere of daylight appears around wherever the
spear is in a 30 ft radius, any creature within the sphere takes
3d8 radiant damage and is blinded. It casts an additional 30 ft
of bright light and 60 ft of dim light from the edge of the
sphere. Any creature that looks directly at it is blinded for
1d4 turns. This can only be used once per day.
Exous Blade
Weapon(longsword), legendary
An unstable blade created from Exous Crystals, it can be
used to absorb spells directed towards its wielder. Each time
it absorbs a spell, it degrades the blade and risks exploding,
releasing any captured spells immediately and dealing 8d8
force damage in a 30 ft radius.
Dagger of the Rogue
Weapon(Dagger), legendary(requires attunement)
A dagger when wielded by a non-rogue, grants the abilities of
a level 1 rogue as if it were a multiclass. As long as you have
the dagger, you can level up your rogue multiclass as normal,
but if the attunement is broken, you lose any levels invested in
the multiclass.
Blade of the Blood Rose
Weapon(Longsword), legendary
A blade with thorns on its hilt, you can feed it your blood by
taking 1d8+CR or character level magical piercing damage to
deal an extra 1d8+CR or character level necrotic damage on
your next attack. You can use this for as many attacks as you
are able, if the damage from using the Blade of the Blood
Rose would reduce you to 0 hitpoints or below, you finish
making the attack, but you start with one death save failed
already, and you make your death saves with disadvantage as
long as the blade is on your person, as it extends its thorns
wanting more blood from your final moments. You can also
drain blood from a dead creature once per body, giving your
next attack 1d4+CR or character level of the creature
necrotic damage.
Axe of the Sun
Weapon(Greataxe), artifact(requires attunement)
An axe capable of absorbing sunlight and storing it, it can
store up to 4 hours of sunlight. For each hour stored, the
wielder can cast flame sphere without concentration, they
can use more hours at one time to cast at a higher spell level
(Casting with 4 hours would count as a fifth level spell.)
Giant's Club
Weapon(Greatclub), rare
If a medium or smaller size creature tries to use this weapon
without the "Powerful Build" or similar ability, they roll melee
attacks with it with disadvantage. While wielding this club,
you have a +2 to Strength and proficiency in athletics, and if
you hit a creature that is medium or smaller size with this
club, you deal an extra 1d4 bludgeoning damage.
Toxic Blade
Weapon(Longsword), very rare
A blade dipped in a poison lake in a toxic plain, its lose all its
ability to cut normally and instead only poisons, dealing
poison damage instead. The poison is so potent that it even
bypasses poison immunity and resistances, and with each cut
on a creature they take 1d4 poison damage for 1d4+1 turns
at the start of their turn. This can stack up to three times. At
level 7 this is increased to four times, at level 12 five times,
and at level 17 six times.

Armors
Armor of the Blood Rose
Armor(Plate), legendary
A plate of armor with thorns on both the inside and the
outside, they can draw in your blood by taking 1d4+CR or
character level magical piercing damage to gain 1/4 of your
CR or character level as AC(rounded down) until the start of
your next turn. If the damage from using Armor of the Blood
Rose would reduce you to 0 hitpoints, you stand upright with
the additional AC until the start of your next turn, at which
point you start with one failed death save already, and and
you make your death saves with disadvantage as long as the
armor is on your person, as it extends its thorns wanting
more blood from your final moments.
Exous Armor
Armor(Plate), legendary(requires attunement)
An extremely powerful armor forged from various Exous
Crystals, it diverts magic along it protecting the wearer like a
lightning rod, providing advantage against all spells cast on
them while wearing it. If a wish spell is cast against the
armor or the person wearing it, the armor will absorb the
spell and shatter, cancelling the spell out.
Armor of Torment
Armor(Plate), legendary
This armor is cursed to torment all who would touch it,
attaching to them permanently unless removed by a wish
spell or similarly powerful magic. This gives a +2 to AC, but
every hour they take 1 necrotic and 1 fire damage. Due to its
constant tormenting nature, rests only restore half the
hitpoints they normally would while wearing the armor. Any
other creatures who stay within 120 ft of the armor for 10
minutes take 1 psychic damage as the armor pulls at their
mind.
Armor of Selflessness
Armor(Plate), legendary
This armor is cursed with a commandment of selflessness,
any who wear it are bound to it, becoming permanently
attached to them unless removed by a wish spell or similarly
powerful magic. Any creature that would take damage within
a 30 ft radius of the person wearing this armor only takes half
damage, the other half of the damage is applied to the person
wearing the armor. If the person wearing this armor dies, the
armor can be removed normally.
Shield of the Blood Rose
Shield(Shield), legendary
A metal shield covered in thorns, whenever a melee attack
fails against you, the target that attacked you takes 1d4+CR
or your character level of necrotic damage, and you regain
hitpoints of the amount dealt.

Wondrous Items
Pulsating Heart
Wondrous item, legendary(requires attunement)
This pulsating human heart can grant the abilities of a level
1 warlock as if it were a multiclass as long as it is in the
person's possession, however attunement requires linking
their life force with the item, and if it is destroyed, they die as
well. If the attunement is broken in any other way, the one
who was attuned to it has their soul ripped out of them and
contained within the heart.
Ring of the Wild
Wondrous item, very rare
A special ring that when worn, grants the Wild Magic
Surge trait, which works on any spellcast or magic weapon
attack. When taking the ring off, roll on the Wild Magic Surge
table for a random effect.
Potion of Permacy
Wondrous item, legendary
Drinking this potion makes any temporary magic effects on
the creature permanent.
Nail of the Planes
Wondrous item, very rare
Can be used to nail anything into place, even nailing things
into thin air, completely immovable unless the nail itself is
removed by a hammer.
Blood Rose
Wondrous item, very rare
A bloody rose with extra sharp and magical thorns,
pricking yourself with it forms a contract to feed it, it drops
and blooms to large scale, and then as the petals fall, nothing
but the Armor, Blade, and Shield of the Blood Rose remain.
Cursed Helm of Darkvision
Wondrous item, rare
A enclosed helmet that grants darkvision of 120 ft, even
through magical darkness, but has a chance the longer a
character wears it to see various hallucinations of various
spirits and ghosts, first just out of sight and scarce, but
getting more and more intense, to the point of eldritch
horrors, dealing 1d8 psychic damage and giving a chance of
insanity. Removing the helmet removes the hallucations, but
putting it on again starts it from the same intensity that the
last person who used it had, and spending too long in too
intense of a state can lead to seeing the hallucinations even
without the helmet on.
Ring of Planes
Wondrous item, artifact
Placing the ring on your finger transports you to a random
plane for one minute, after which the ring will transport back
to the plane you put it on. If the creature takes off the ring
before the one minute is up, or if its no longer on the
creature, the ring will transport itself back without the
creature.
Loaded Die
Wondrous item, rare
A magic d6 die, the one who throws it knows the result
before it lands, and can will it to be either +1 or -1 what the
result would be.
Exous Crystal
Wondrous item, very rare
This magic crystal can be used to absorb a spell directed
towards its wielder, and stored for later use as an action. Only
one spell can be stored at a time. You can use this multiple
times, but each time it is used it degrades and risks
exploding, releasing any captured spell immediately and
dealing 4d8 force damage in a 30 ft radius.
Chalice of the Undying
Wondrous item, artifact
This chalice grants immortality of the body to any whose
blood is put in it as long as the blood remains in the chalice.
This can be done for any number of creatures. If the
creature's blood is removed from the chalice, or the chalice is
destroyed, the creature dies and cannot be resurrected by
anything other than divine intervention.
Helm of Omniscience
Wondrous item, very rare
Blinds all of the wearers other senses, grants 120 ft of
truesight and advantage on insight checks.
Seven Leaf Clover
Wondrous item, artifact
Grants extraordinary luck in all endeavours, you'll always win
in gambling or other games of random chance, but the longer
you use it, the more attention it attracts.
Gourd of Goodberries
Wondrous item, rare
A gourd filled with the juice from goodberries, it can be
drunk from once per day to completely nourish and hydrate
as well as restore 2 HP. At the start of a new day, the gourd
refills itself.
God of Craftsman's Hammer
Wondrous item, artifact
A hammer which is able to shape the material world
around it, it can move any material, shape it in any way, push
it or pull it, even with the ability to make entire mountains.
Mortals have trouble wielding it, suffering increasing levels of
exhaustion even to the point of death if they try to move too
much at one time or too much in one day.
Kitsune Tail
Wondrous item, very rare
A severed tail from a Kitsune said to grant luck, it has a
curse placed upon it by the Kitsune god to age anyone who
grasps it that isn't a Kitsune to age to a elder state (whatever
is considered old for their race). Has no effect on immortal
creatures.
Amnesiac's Cursed Ring
Wondrous item, rare
A forgetful ring that forgot what its supposed to curse you
with, it'll constantly ask you what its curse is and won't come
off until you answer. Once you answer, it'll be like "Oh yeah!"
and make the curse happen, and then leave your finger.
