▒░        Using  This  Document        ░▒  
 
  
This  is  an  experimental  playtest  document  -  the  functions  listed  within  are  not  yet  finalized.  The  goal  is  for   
Kill  Sector  players  and  GM’s  from  around  the  world  to  try  these  out  on  their  characters  and  in  their  gauntlets,  in   
order  to  see  if  they’re  not  only  balanced,  but  also  fun  to  use.   
  
This  book  features  functions  revolving  around  three  concepts:  chemical  weapons,  candle  magic,  and   
catapults.  Chemical  weapons  are  super-potent  biochemicals  that  affect  anyone  that  breathes  them  in,  injects  them,   
or  otherwise  has  them  introduced  into  the  body;  you  define  your  chemical  weapon  by  choosing  a  specific  chemical   
and  then  a  specific  way  to  distribute  said  chemical,  with  over  a  hundred  combinations  between  choosing  your   
chemical  and  distribution  method.  Candle  magic  is  a  complex  set  of  magical  functions,  which  you  can  use  to  stake   
out  areas  of  the  map  and  alter  the  rules  within  those  spaces;  they  can  let  you  do  anything  from  healing  to  increasing   
accuracy  of  certain  weapons  to  changing  how  damage  works  to  altering  the  very  means  of  motion  within  those   
spaces.  Catapults  are  massive  warmachines  of  the  ancient  world  that  let  you  fling  game-changing  payloads  across   
the  map;  the  default  catapult  comes  with  boulders,  but  these  boulders  can  be  swapped  out  for  one  of  over  a  dozen   
alternate  specialized  payloads  -  anything  from  the  Arctic  Shard  that  changes  the  battlefield  to  a  frozen  hellscape,  to   
the  Infiltrator  Capsule,  which  lets  you  launch  one  of  your  buddies  deep  into  enemy  territory  in  a  hollowed-out   
cannonball.   
  
All  three  of  these  categories  have  a  very  specific  set  of  mechanics  with  deep  customization  options;  exactly   
the  kind  of  thing  that  is  best  playtested  en  masse,  with  people  bringing  their  different  perspectives  to  use  and   
combine  these  functions  in  ways  we  never  would  have  anticipated.

2  pt  ///Chemical  Weapon   
One  of  a  deadly  array  of  horrific  implements   
of  chemical  warfare.  These  are  potent,  limited-use   
weapons  specifically  crafted  to  grievously  wound 
organic  entities  or  otherwise  affect  the  body.   
You  cannot  apply  weapon  modifiers  to   
chemical  weapons;  they  are  bespoke  substances  that   
do  not  operate  like  normal  weaponry.   
All  of  these  chemicals  are  biological  effects;   
thus,  they  are  incapable  of  affecting  machines  or   
similar.   
Many  of  these  chemicals  rely  on  their   
targets  to  breathe  them  in.  Someone  can  hold  their   
breath  as  a  free  action  or  reaction;  they  cannot  react   
to  hold  their  breath  to  something  they  did  not  detect   
before  inhaling.  If  someone  is  within  the  influence  of   
a  chemical  when  it’s  released,  and  the  chemical  is  not   
clearly  visible,  they  can  make  a  perception  check  at   
that  moment  to  attempt  to  detect  it;  if  they  fail,  they   
inhale  it.   
Holding  one’s  breath  for  an  extended  period   
of  time  may  lead  to  suffocation.  If  they  take  damage,   
they  might  end  up  inhaling  as  a  reflex.  More   
information  on  holding  one’s  breath  can  be  found  in   
the  rules  section  o  fthe  core  rulebook.   
When  you  take  this  function,  you  must   
choose  a  specific  chemical  and  a  specific  means  of   
dispersal.  Different  chemicals  and  means  of  dispersal   
are  useful  in  different  scenarios  -  carefully  consider   
which  ones  best  suit  your  intended  usage.   
  
Chemicals   
  
Bedlam  D   
A  biochemical  vapor  that  destroys  all  sense   
of  trust  and  turns  everyone  against  one-another.   
Bedlam  D  is  clearly  visible.  If  someone   
inhales  the  gas,  they  take  an  affliction  100  to  become   
overwhelmed  by  paranoia  until  the  end  of  the  wave.   
They  cannot  treat  anyone  as  an  ally  or  neutral  party;   
they  must  treat  everyone  equally  as  enemies.   
Furthermore,  they  must  attack  the  nearest  enemy  with   
whatever  means  necessary.   
If  the  affliction  fails  against  someone,  they   
are  immune  to  Bedlam  D  for  the  rest  of  the  gauntlet.   
If  the  affliction  succeeds  against  someone,  they  are   
affected  by  all  Bedlam  D  for  the  rest  of  the  gauntlet.   
  
Geist   
A  nigh-undetectable  mist  of  quasi-photons   
that  release  devastating  payloads  of  energy  once   
introduced  to  the  respiratory  system.   
Geist  is  extremely  hard  to  detect  (DC  90   
perception  to  smell  from  up  to  6  squares  away,  DC  40   
with  the  Scent  function  or  similar).  If  someone  enters 
the  gas  or  begins  their  turn  within  the  gas,  and  they   
breathe  it  in,  they  take  average  beam  damage  as  the   
quasi-photons  burst  within  them.  If  someone  did  not   
detect  the  gas  before  breathing  it  in,  they  cannot   
protect  themselves  by  stating  that  they  hold  their   
breath.   
  
Gourmatron   
An  odorous,  slightly  magical  chemical  that   
emulates  extremely  pleasant  food  scents.   
Gourmatron  is  clearly  visible.  If  someone   
inhales  the  gas,  they  take  an  affliction  150  to  smell   
what  they  believe  to  be  their  favorite  food  or  similar   
nutrient  base  (if  inhaled  by  a  player,  have  them   
describe  what  that  is).  If  they  do  not  ingest  food  in  a   
typical  fashion,  but  can  still  be  affected  by  biological   
effects,  then  they  smell  something  else  pleasant.   
They’ll  be  subconsciously  driven  to  stay  within  the   
smell,  even  against  their  better  judgement.  They  will   
not  willingly  move  outside  of  the  influence  of   
Gourmatron  for  the  rest  of  the  gauntlet  for  as  long  as   
it  is  present.   
If  the  affliction  fails  against  someone,  they   
are  immune  to  Gourmatron  for  the  rest  of  the   
gauntlet.  If  the  affliction  succeeds  against  someone,   
they  are  affected  by  all  Gourmatron  for  the  rest  of  the   
gauntlet.   
  
Hallucinorg   
A  generalized  psychedelic  capable  of   
producing  potent  hallucinations.  There  are  as  many   
strains  of  Hallucinorg  as  there  are  recreational  users.

When  you  take  this  option,  name  your  strain   
and  decide  the  general  nature  of  the  hallucinations  -   
what  kinds  of  things  people  might  sense  while   
experiencing  it,  and  what  kinds  of  emotions  they

you.  Hallucinorg  cannot  be  used  to  detect  something   
in  the  real  world  that  you  couldn’t  detect  without  it;  it   
doesn’t  enhance  your  senses  in  any  way.   
As  an  example  strain,  Diamond  Epithet   
causes  someone  to  feel  a  sense  of  poetic  bliss  as  they   
traverse  a  world  of  crystal  forms,  rolling  mists,  and   
death  imagery.  As  another  example,  Zing-Morph   
causes  you  to  see  everyone  as  an  anthropomorphic   
creature  (part  human,  part  animal);  if  someone  has  a   
specific  animal-like  form  they  prefer  to  be  seen  as,   
and  they  state  as  much  while  you’re  on  Zing-Morph,   
you  will  see  them  as  such.  As  a  third  example, 
Symphonorg  MoonSonat  causes  someone  to  very   
vividly  hear  Beethoven’s  Moonlight  Sonata  in   
exquisite  fidelity,  at  the  exclusion  of  all  other  sound.   
As  a  final  example,  Tony  Ska’s  Excellent  Vapor   
causes  someone  to  see  black  and  white  checkerboards   
on  walls  and  other  flat  surfaces,  and  whenever   
someone  speaks  they  always  sound  like  they’re   
speaking  in  perfect  rhythm  with  musical  horns   
backing  them.   
Hallucinorg  is  difficult  to  detect  (DC  75   
perception  to  smell  from  up  to  6  squares  away,  DC  25   
with  the  Scent  function  or  similar).  If  someone   
inhales  the  gas,  they  take  an  affliction  25  to  succumb   
to  its  hallucinatory  nature;  for  the  rest  of  the  wave,   
that  character’s  sensory  input  is  completely  at  the   
GM’s  mercy,  so  long  as  what  the  GM  describes  is   
within  the  parameters  of  the  given  strain  of   
Hallucinorg  you  defined.  If  the  affliction  fails  by  25   
or  less,  they  may  still  experience  a  minor  version  of   
the  hallucination,  at  GM  discretion.  If  someone  did   
not  detect  the  gas  before  breathing  it  in,  they  cannot   
protect  themselves  by  stating  that  they  hold  their   
breath.  Someone  can  reroll  the  perception  check  to   
detect  Halucinorg  if  its  affliction  fails  against  them.   
If  the  affliction  fails  against  someone,  the   
target  takes  one  stack  of  intake.  Intake  wears  off  at   
the  end  of  the  wave  it  was  applied.  For  each  stack  of   
intake  someone  has,  Hallucinorg  has  +25  on  its   
affliction  rolls  against  them.   
  
Hellsmoke   
A  thick  biochemical  gas  that  becomes   
dangerously  exothermic  once  introduced  to  the   
respiratory  system.   
Hellsmoke  is  clearly  visible  and  opaque,   
blocking  vision  through  more  than  three  squares  of   
the  smoke.  If  someone  enters  the  gas  or  begins  their   
turn  within  the  gas,  and  they  breathe  it  in,  they  take   
strong  fire  damage  as  it  burns  up  their  insides.   
  
Lethargon   
A  medical  substance  that  rapidly  heals  the   
body  at  the  cost  of  running  the  patient  ragged.   
Lethargon  is  clearly  visible.  If  someone   
enters  the  gas  or  begins  their  turn  within  the  gas,  and   
they  breathe  it  in,  they  medically  heal  HP  equal  to   
half  their  max  HP,  and  medically  heal  25  HP  at  the   
beginning  of  each  of  their  turns  until  the  end  of  the   
wave;  however,  until  the  end  of  the  wave,  they  take   
-25  to  evasion  and  rolls  to  hit,  and  they  cannot   
perform  perception  or  knowledge  checks.   
Multiple  doses  of  Lethargon  do  not  stack  the   
rest-of-wave  effects;  someone  who  consumes  two   
doses  of  Lethargon  do  not  heal  50  HP  per  turn  at  the   
cost  of  taking  -50  to  evasion  and  rolls  to  hit.   
However,  the  healing-half-of-max-HP  effect  can 
occur  multiple  times  in  a  wave  if  consumed  multiple   
times.   
  
Lungrot   
Initially-neutral  chemicals  that  turn  into   
deadly  corrosives  once  introduced  to  the  respiratory   
system.   
Lungrot  is  clearly  visible.  If  someone  enters   
the  gas  or  begins  their  turn  within  the  gas,  and  they   
breathe  it  in,  they  take  very  strong  acid  damage  as  it   
melts  their  innards.

a  pickaxe  and  mine  for  gold”)  to  implant  into  the

Mind  Haze.  You  can  implant  a  different  command   
into  the  unreleased  chemical  as  a  standard  action.   
Mind  Haze  is  somewhat  subtle  (DC  50   
perception  to  smell  from  up  to  6  squares  away,

taken  over  by  the  haze  until  the  end  of  the  wave.   
Their  first  priority  is  following  the  command  you

If  the  affliction  fails  against  someone,  they   
are  immune  to  that  particular  cloud  of  Mind  Haze  for   
the  rest  of  the  gauntlet.   
  
Minus  Zero   
A  tricky-to-detect  biochemical  vapor  that   
becomes  dangerously  endothermic  once  introduced  to   
the  respiratory  system.   
Minus  Zero  is  somewhat  subtle  (DC  50   
perception  to  smell  from  up  to  6  squares  away,   
auto-succeed  with  the  Scent  function  or  similar).  If   
someone  enters  the  gas  or  begins  their  turn  within  the   
gas,  and  they  breathe  it  in,  they  take  strong  cold   
damage  as  it  freezes  up  their  internal  organs.  If   
someone  did  not  detect  the  gas  before  breathing  it  in,   
they  cannot  protect  themselves  by  stating  that  they   
hold  their  breath.   
  
Pheromones   
Pleasant-smelling  chemicals  that  enchant   
those  that  inhale  it.  The  pheromones  are  imprinted   
with  subconsciously-planted  mental  images  of  your   
likeness.   
Pheromones  are  clearly  visible.  If  someone   
inhales  the  gas,  they  take  affliction  125  to  become   
enchanted  by  you  until  the  end  of  the  wave.  They   
treat  you  in  every  way  as  an  ally  instead  of  an  enemy.   
They  have  a  will  of  their  own,  and  so  will  not   
automatically  follow  any  command  you  give  -  but   
they  will  consider  the  commands  as  if  given  by  an   
ally.  This  enchantment  breaks  if  they  witness  you   
attempt  to  harm  them  or  one  of  their  allies  or   
otherwise  show  yourself  as  a  direct  threat  to  them.   
  If  the  affliction  fails  against  someone,  they   
are  immune  to  pheromones  with  your  imprint  for  the   
rest  of  the  gauntlet.  If  the  affliction  succeeds  against   
someone,  they  are  affected  by  all  pheromones  with   
your  imprint  for  the  rest  of  the  gauntlet.   
  
Rejectovex 
An  odious,  nauseating  chemical  that  the   
body  forcefully  rejects  once  inhaled.   
Rejectovex  is  clearly  visible.  If  someone   
enters  the  gas  or  begins  their  turn  within  the  gas,  and   
they  breathe  it  in,  they  take  an  affliction  125  to  have   
their  body  forcefully  reject  the  chemical.  They  must   
spend  the  first  available  move  action  expelling  the   
chemical  by  whatever  means  their  species  does  so   
(typically  via  oral  expulsion).  If  the  affliction   
succeeds  by  50  or  more,  they  spend  the  first  available   
standard  action  instead.  When  the  chemical  is   
expelled,  they  also  take  miniscule  poison  damage.   
Expelling  Rejectovex  provokes  attacks  of   
opportunity.   
If  someone  takes  on  multiple  doses  at  once   
(for  example,  via  consuming  multiple  Rejectovex   
pills),  then  they  go  through  each  dose  one  at  a  time.  If   
someone  takes  three  doses,  for  example,  they  lose   
their  next  three  move  actions  to  expulsion.  You  do   
not  need  to  cash  in  other  actions  in  order  to  expel   
multiple  times  in  the  same  turn  unless  you  wish  to  do   
so.   
If  someone  expels  multiple  doses   
consecutively  without  a  round  in-between,  the   
damage  taken  by  the  expulsion  goes  up  one  damage   
rank  during  each  consecutive  expulsion.  If  someone   
expels  for  three  rounds  in  a  row,  for  example,  they   
take  miniscule  poison  damage  for  the  first  expulsion,   
then  very  weak  poison  damage  for  the  second   
expulsion,  then  weak  damage  for  the  third  expulsion.   
The  damage  resets  to  miniscule  if  there  is  a  full  round   
where  they  do  not  expel  at  all.

of  the  chemical.  They  must  spend  all  actions   
available  to  avoid  being  within  the  influence  of   
Shudder.

Tear  Gas   
An  infamous  visual  irritant.  Tear  gas  is   
clearly  visible  and  opaque,  blocking  vision  through   
more  than  three  squares  of  the  smoke.  If  someone   
enters  the  gas  or  begins  their  turn  within  the  gas,  and   
their  eyes  are  exposed  to  it,  they  take  an  affliction   
100  to  be  blinded  until  the  beginning  of  their  next   
turn  (-25  on  all  to-hit  rolls,  -25  evasion,  DC  50   
coordination  or  perception  to  move  in  intended   
direction  instead  of  a  random  direction,  cannot  make   
vision-based  perception  checks)  as  their  eyes  water   
uncontrollably.   
  
Trance   
A  psychedelic  that  manifests  as  rhythmic   
visual  hallucinations.  It  can  have  either  positive  or   
negative  effects,  depending  on  whether  or  not  an   
negadote  is  within  the  body.   
No  matter  what  dispersal  option  you  choose   
to  take  with  Trance,  it  also  comes  with  a  bottle  of   
fifty  pills  of  the  negadote.   
Trance  is  clearly  visible.  If  someone  enters   
the  gas  or  begins  their  turn  within  the  gas,  and  they   
breathe  it  in,  they  take  -15  to  evasion  until  the   
beginning  of  their  next  turn;  if  they  have  consumed   
the  negadote  at  any  point  in  the  gauntlet  prior  to   
inhalation,  then  they  take  +15  to  evasion  instead.   
  
Tranq   
A  potent  sleeping  gas.  The  effects  might  be   
delayed,  but  it’ll  likely  take  just  about  anyone.   
When  taking  Tranq,  choose  for  how  long  the 
sleep  lasts;  it  can  be  anywhere  from  one  round  to   
twenty-four  hours.   
Tranq  is  clearly  visible.  If  someone  enters   
the  gas  or  begins  their  turn  within  the  gas,  and  they   
breathe  it  in,  they  take  affliction  50  to  fall  asleep   
where  they  stand.  They  fall  prone,  cannot  evade   
attacks  or  perform  actions,  and  can  be  targeted  by  a   
coup-de-grace.  They  wake  up  as  soon  as  they  take   
damage.  If  someone  sleeps  for  at  least  four   
consecutive  hours  under  the  effects  of  Tranq,  they  are   
healed  to  full  HP,  and  any  limited-use-per-gauntlet   
functions  they  have  are  restored  to  full  usage.   
If  the  affliction  fails  against  someone,  the   
target  takes  one  stack  of  fatigue.  Fatigue  wears  off  at   
the  end  of  the  wave  it  was  applied.  For  each  stack  of   
fatigue  someone  has,  they  take  -2  to  all  rolls,  -2  to   
evasion,  and  sleep-based  afflictions  (like  Tranq)  take   
+25  against  them.   
  
Ultra-Sense   
A  performance-enhancing  substance  that   
destroys  the  body  while  unlocking  untapped  sensory   
potentials.  Unknown  colors  are  seen,  unheard  sounds   
heard,  unknown  horizons  crossed.

of  a  magical  area.  Each  candle  represents  a  point  of   
the  polygon  within  which  the  magic  area  resides.  The   
magical  properties  of  the  area  are  dependent  on   
which  candles  are  used  to  form  it.  Multiple  candles  of   
the  same  type  stack.  In  order  for  the  magic  to  work,   
each  candle  must  be  no  further  than  6  squares  from   
two  other  candles  in  the  perimeter,  you  must  be   
within  the  perimeter,  and  you  must  be  alive.  Adding   
more  candles  to  the  perimeter  of  an  existing  area  adds   
to  the  effect  of  that  area.   
Candles  can  be  knocked  over  or   
extinguished  as  a  swift  action.  When  a  candle  is   
knocked  over  or  extinguished,  it  is  discounted  from   
the  area.   
You  have  a  number  of  magic  candles  equal   
to  1  plus  double  the  number  of  points  invested  in  this   
function  (1  point  of  investment  means  3  candles,  2   
points  means  5,  3  points  means  7,  et  cetera).  You  can   
have  multiple  candles  of  the  same  type.   
Multiple  candle  magicians  can  form  a  shared   
magic  area  made  by  candles  from  their  combined   
collections  -  if  this  is  done,  each  candle  magician  that   
contributed  at  least  one  candle  is  considered  an   
“owner”  of  the  area.

1.
Choose  a  2-by-2  square  target  area  to  hit;  the

boulder  lands  relative  to  its  intended

intended  location.  On  a  5  or  6,  move  the

the  topmost  point  of  the  d10  points.  On  a  3   
or  4,  move  the  target  area  6  squares  away  in   
the  direction  the  topmost  point  of  the  d10   
points.  On  a  1  or  2,  move  the  target  12   
squares  away  in  the  direction  the  topmost   
point  of  the  d10  points.   
3.
Apply  the  following  attack  to  the  target  area:   
2x2  square  area  /  +25  /  lethal  bludgeoning   
Weapon  modifiers  can  be  applied  to  the  catapult  at   
double  the  cost..  Other  objects  can  be  loaded  into  the
