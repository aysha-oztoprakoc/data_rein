Apothecary (Archetype)
Apothecaries are plaguewrights who bristle at their very
moniker, delving instead into the healing arts in an effort
to
distance
themselves
from
their
less­benevolent
colleagues.
Apothecaries have the following class features:
Do No Harm: Apothecaries largely shun research into
malignant microbes and focus on curatives instead. As a
result, all cultures present in an apothecary’s vials must
use a benign strain.
Dosage Pool (Su): At 1st level, an apothecary gains a
dosage pool, a measure of his ability to add medication to
his cultures. The number of points in the apothecary’s
dosage pool is equal to his apothecary level. Whenever the
apothecary draws a syringe of culture from one of his
vials, he may spend a dosage point. If he does, he selects
any one terminal mutation that he knows and treats the
newly­drawn
syringe
as
though
that
mutation
were
present in its culture. He must add benign mutations to
benign cultures and malignant mutations to malignant
cultures, as normal.
The dosage pool is replenished each morning after 8
hours of rest or meditation; these hours do not need to be
consecutive.
Terminal Euphoria (Su): At 1st level, all of the
apothecary’s benign cultures gain the following mutation.
It does not occupy a mutation slot when gained in this
manner. At 4th level and every three apothecary levels
thereafter, all of his benign cultures gain the mutation an
additional time, to a maximum of seven times at 19th
level.
Terminal Euphoria (Benign, Terminal)
When a strain with this mutation dies, its final act is to
release a number of chemicals that act as a potent
healing factor.
Effect: When a culture containing this mutation ends,
the subject is healed for 1 point of damage. This mutation
can be taken multiple times. Each time, the subject heals
for an additional point of damage.
This ability replaces discerning eye.
Medicine Man (Su): At 20th level, if one of the
apothecary’s cultures has had a terminal mutation added
to it one or more times, then he treats that terminal
mutation as though it were taken an additional time. This
has no effect on terminal mutations that cannot be taken
multiple times.
This ability replaces forced evolution visionary.
New Mutations
Terminal Bravado (Benign)
Requires: Plaguewright 8
This microbe's apoptosis produces a spurt of adrenaline.
Effect: When a culture containing this mutation ends,
the subject may make a new saving throw at the original
DC against each fear effect affecting it. If this new saving
throw is successful, the fear effect ends immediately.
Terminal Clarity (Benign)
Requires: Plaguewright 10
For just an instant, everything becomes clear.
Effect: When a culture containing this mutation ends,
the subject’s weapon attacks ignore up to 2 points of
damage reduction for 1 round. This mutation can be taken
multiple
times.
Each
time,
the
subject
ignores
an
additional 2 points of damage reduction. This does not
apply to damage reduction without a type (DR/­).
Terminal Rehabilitation (Benign)
Requires: Apothecary 10
Properly engineered microbes can bring an individual
back from death’s door.
Effect: When a culture containing this mutation ends,
choose an ability score. The subject is healed for 1 point of
damage to the chosen ability score.
New Feats
Chaser Coating (Vial)
You add a colony of microorganisms to the coating of a
vial.
Prerequisite: Containers class feature, plaguewright
level 9th
Benefit: When taking this feat, select both a vial, as well
as a mutation you know that is both benign and
malignant. Cultures made in the chosen vial have the
chosen mutation added to them without occupying a
mutation slot. You cannot choose mutations that take up
two or more mutation slots in this manner.
Special: This feat can be taken multiple times. Each
time, select a different vial. If a vial to which one or more
feats have been applied is lost, then its replacement
automatically gets the benefits of whatever feats were
applied to the original.
Vein Searcher
You are adept at finding the weak point in any creature’s
hide, so long as they stand still for you.
Prerequisite: Containers class feature
Benefit: Your syringes ignore the damage reduction
of willing creatures.
