Magical Kitties 
Save the Day!
You are cute. You are cunning. You are fierce. 
You are a magical kitty, and it’s time to save the day!
When your human’s favorite doll mysteriously goes 
missing, it’s up to you to bring it home. But you’ll have 
to brave a haunted house and foil the schemes of hyper-
intelligent racoons!
This action-packed comic book is also a full adventure for 
the Magical Kitties Save the Day RPG. It lets you pick 
your own path in the story.
Find out more at www.atlas-games.com/magicalkitties!
