Keys of Mystery (Wondrous Item) 
 
Aura: Teleportation  
 
Caster Level: 5 
 
Slot: Pack   
 
Price: 4,000 GP + 1,000 GP per key, up to 
4 keys.  
 
Weight: 1 lb. 
Description 
 
The Keys of Mystery are a key ring with up 
to 4 keys on it.  The keys will always fit any 
non-magical lock. When used to open any 
locked door, they will automatically succeed 
in unlocking that door.  When the door is 
opened, it will open into a random room 
within 10 miles radius.  That room must also 
have a door that locks. If no rooms are 
available it will simply open the door.   
 
If the door is unlocked, locking it with the 
Keys of Mystery will cause the next person 
unlocking it to experience the magical 
effect. 
 
The Keys of Mystery appear as heavy black 
Iron keys on a black iron ring.   
Construction 
 
Requirements Craft Wondrous Item, 
Teleportation; Cost 2,000 GP + 500 GP per 
key.

Helm of Happiness (Cursed Item) 
 
Aura: Enchantment 
 
Caster Level: 4 
 
Slot: Head   
 
Price: --- 
 
Weight: 5 lbs. 
Description 
 
The Helm of Happiness was thought to 
have been created as an item of revenge.  
Several hundred years ago a group of 
wizards found themselves first ejected from 
their table and then ejected from their Pub 
by a group of rowdy mercenaries. After a 
huddled conversation, a bit of light thievery 
and a late night magical ritual, the Helm of 
Happiness was created.   
 
Appearing like a beautiful and functional 
piece of armor, the Helm of Happiness 
glows with Enchantment.   
 
When the Helm of Happiness is donned it 
reduces the target’s mental facilities.  The 
target’s Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma 
are immediately reduced by 1d6 points.  
The target’s abilities cannot drop below 1. 
 
In addition to this effect, the target will 
instantly find themselves in a happy mood, 
at peace with the world around them and 
willing to smile at even the grossest injustice 
to their person. 
 
They will also forget ever having donned the 
helm, and cannot perceive it by any 
manner. 
 
The Helm can only be removed by the 
target. 
 
 
 
The target regains 1 point to 1 ability every 
day they wear the helm until they are 
restored to normal at which point they will 
perceive the helm and can willingly remove 
it. 
Creation 
 
Magic Items Helm of Brilliance

Insult to Injury (Magical Weapon) 
 
Aura: Conjuration 
 
Caster Level: 2 
 
Slot: Hand  
 
Price: 1,000 GP 
 
Weight: 1 lb. 
 
Magical Knife: 1d4 +1 
Description 
 
This curious magical blade appears to be 
only a +1 knife.  However, on a successful 
critical hit, in addition to the target taking 
damage, two gallons of water is conjured 
over the targets head and immediately falls 
on them.  The target then has to make a 
Reflex save (DC 15) or automatically lose 
initiative on the next round. 
 
Additionally, if the wielder has a critical miss 
(rolls a 1 on a d20) two gallons of water is 
immediately dumped on the wielder’s head 
and they must make the same save. 
 
Construction 
 
Requirements Craft Magical Arms and 
Armor, Create Water; Cost 500 GP.

Assassin’s Gambit (Wondrous Item) 
 
Aura: Necromancy 
 
Caster Level: 3 
 
Slot: Hand  
 
Price: 15,000 GP 
 
Weight: 1 lb. 
 
Magical Knife: 1d4 +2 
Description 
 
Assassin’s Gambit was created as a 
weapon to inflict a maximum amount of 
damage on one target.   
 
When the wielder hits a target with the 
Assassin’s Gambit and scores a critical hit 
or a successful sneak attack they can 
choose to either inflict the normal damage 
or invoke the Gambit. 
 
If the wielder chooses to invoke the Gambit, 
the target takes no damage from the 
successful critical hit or sneak attack that 
round. 
 
The wielder then cannot attack as a 
standard or full round action for the next two 
rounds.  At the beginning of the fourth round 
before any other action and regardless of 
any initiative rolls, the target takes the 
maximum amount of damage multiplied by 
two. 
 
For example:  If a 5th level Rogue 
successfully sneak attacks a target with the 
Assassin’s Gambit at the beginning of the 
fourth round they would inflict 48 points of 
damage.  (3d6 maximum = 18 points, plus 
1d4 maximum = 4 points, plus 2 points 
magical damage = 24 points.  Multiplied by  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
two, this is 48 points of damage).  If the 
wielder has any Strength modifiers or other  
 
damage modifiers they would add that to 
the total.   
Construction 
 
Requirements Craft Wondrous Item, Bleed, 
Crafter must be able to inflict at least 5d6 
points of damage in a sneak attack; Cost 
7,500 GP

Stein of Frothy Ale (Wondrous Item) 
 
Aura: Conjuration 
 
Caster Level: 3 
 
Slot: Hand  
 
Price: 500 GP 
 
Weight: 1 lb. 
Description 
 
The Stein of Frothy Ale has long been a 
traditional Dwarven party favor.   
 
This overlarge stein is always decorative 
and generally features royal crests, images 
of battles or burly bearded Dwarves. 
 
Once per day, the Stein of Frothy Ale can 
summon into itself 2 pints of cold, frothy ale.  
This ale is always dark and always of the 
highest quality. 
 
Dwarven mythology holds that in the far 
mountains of a distant Dwarven holding is 
the most bewildered Dwarven brewer in 
history. 
Construction 
 
Requirements Craft Wondrous Item, 
Create Food and Water; Cost 250 GP
