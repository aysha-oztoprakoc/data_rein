Introduction
Welcome to Krampus Cane: a magical weapon be­
queathed by the evil entity known as Krampus! 
About this Supplement
There are two sections to this brief supplement. 
This first section describes more about progressing 
weapons, progression rules, attunement, and other rules 
and mechanics specific to this supplement.
The second section contains a Weapon of Lore called 
Krampus Cane: a magic weapon that increases in pow­
er as the attuned creature gains experience. This section 
also includes the weapon’s Lore, Location, Encounter 
Ideas, Progression Rules, and the weapon’s Progressing 
Statistics.
Encounter Ideas
To help you introduce the weapon into your game, we’ve 
included several encounter ideas.
Attunement
Since Weapons of Lore increase in power based on the 
experience level or Challenge Rating (CR) of the crea­
tures who wield them, attunement is required, even for 
weapons of the Common rarity. Each weapon typically 
attunes at the Common level, no matter the experience 
level of its wielder. However, you may adjust this as you 
like, allowing a higher-level character to acquire the weap­
on and have it attune at the Uncommon, Rare, Very Rare, 
or Legendary level. We do suggest the weapon’s rarity stay 
within the character’s experience range, as listed below.

No Advancement Required
You may decide to reward a Krampus Cane to a character 
and choose not to allow the item to advance in power. To 
do so, simply choose the rarity you want to assign, and 
then provide that information to the character and player. 
Assigning Weapons to NPCs
We encourage you to give your next villain a Krampus 
Cane. If you do, to determine the rarity of the weapon 
they wield, use the creature’s or NPC’s CR and refer to 
the Creature CR of the above table.
Weapon Advancement
For the weapon to increase in power, it must first meet the 
progression requirement, and then the attuned creature 
must finish a long rest. When they awake from their rest, 
the weapon has increased in power.
Increasing Power
In most cases, each Weapon of Lore increases in power, 
meaning it retains the Common and Uncommon prop­
erties when it advances to the Rare rarity, and so on. In 
other cases, a power may be replaced, or it may change. 
For instance, weapons that have a +1 bonus to attack and 
damage rolls may improve upon this bonus, but they do 
not stack. 
For example, Krampus Cane begins as a Common 
item with the Stench of Death property. This property 
does nothing other than cause the weapon to release the 
stench of death and decay. However, when the weapon 
reaches Uncommon rarity, on a hit it deals an extra 1d6 
necrotic damage to any creature who can smell the stench. 
In addition, the attack and damage bonuses change 
as the weapon progresses. At Uncommon, the weapon 
has a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls. When it pro­
gresses to Rare, the bonus to attack and damage rolls 
increases to +2.

Krampus Cane
While Santa dispenses gifts of hope and joy, Krampus be­
queaths these vile canes. 
Lore
It is said that Krampus, the horned anthropomorphic fig­
ure who often stands opposite Santa during the winter 
holidays, grants these magical canes to those who believe 
in him. However, one must be worthy of such a gift, fol­
lowing evil traditions and of a non-good nature.
Location
There are several Krampus Canes throughout the realm. 
While a few locations are listed in the following En­
counter Ideas, the weapon can be found nearly any­
where, including being wielded by an unruly, intoxicated 
bar patron.
Encounter Ideas
•	 A Gift from Krampus. While good-hearted ad­
venturers may be visited by Santa, Krampus visits 
those with an evil heart, bequeathing them either a 
magic item to help them in their struggles against 
good, or a Krampus Cane. A particularly evil ad­
venturer may be visited by Krampus while their 
companions celebrate Christmas.
•	 Museum. The characters notice a Krampus Cane 
in a local museum. If they don’t recognize the weap­
on and know its lore, an archmage does, and steals 
the weapon from the museum.
•	 Mother Krampus. Mother Krampus is an evil 
night hag who despises Christmas and enjoys 
kidnapping children on Christmas Eve. Krampus, 
approving of the hag’s actions, gave her a Krampus 
Cane.
Progression
Krampus Canes increase in power as the attuned crea­
ture’s proficiency bonus increases.
Statistics
Weapon (Club), Rarity Varies (Requires Attunement)
Common. Stench of Death. While holding this magic 
weapon, can use an Action to speak its command word, 
causing it to emit the foul stench of death and decay for 
1 minute. The smell is noticeable by any creature within 
60 feet of you that can smell. Once used, you can’t use this 
property again until you finish a long rest.
Uncommon. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and dam­
age rolls made with this magic weapon. In addition, while 
Stench of Death is active, attacks made with this weapon 
deal an extra 2d6 necrotic damage.
Rare. The bonus to attack and damage rolls increases 
to +2, and the extra necrotic damage dealt by Stench 
of Death increases to 3d6. Stench of Death can now be 
activated with a Bonus Action, its duration increases to 
5 minutes, and it recharges when you finish a short or 
long rest.
Very Rare. Hooved & Horned. As an Action, you can 
speak the weapon’s command word to activate one of 
the following features, each of which has a duration of 2 
hours. Once used, you can’t use this property again until 
you finish a short or long rest.
Polymorph. You can cast the polymorph spell (self 
only) from the weapon to transform into a goat. When
