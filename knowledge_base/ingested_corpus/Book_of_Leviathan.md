While doctrine, both historic and inked on fresh paper, has long suppressed CAIN’s
opponents to the fringes of esotericism, in many hushed whispers does the curious
exorcist hear of inextinguishable dissent. Under//Heaven does not exist, not in any official
capacity worth teaching the of CAIN’s psychic playthings and sin-filled soldiers. These
whispers are not from a place under heaven however, but instead echoes long grazed on
the grasses of Eden.
Any number of names that may come to describe that which lurks ever watchful in CAIN’s
shadow, pulling its many noticed strings take many names. Aware Eden, Judas, Tartarus, Samael. All
identified is broadly swept under one identifiable label. A ‘organisation’ known as
Leviathan.
Leviathan, if thought to be an organisation in any capacity, is said to be under the direction
of the elusive Lion//Lamb, some conduit for higher nonhuman forces. What is thought by
many to be an ascendant Imago, and known by few to be something even worse. An
empire of secrets hidden in many intangible thoughts, and the twisting shadows of the
capital hellscape upon the waking world. Grown in the dark in the trembling sinseeds of
those it moulds, breaks and shapes, like clay upon a great, gnashing wheel. If the
Leviathan is a sum of smaller parts, it is an organisation even more indeterminable than
CAIN in its shape, in its motives, and in its desires.
It is said, that the belief of Leviathan that the concepts of Sin and Grace need to be
conjoined as one. That the psychic veil that layers itself upon the world needs to be torn,
and pulled over the waking form of reality. That the worldly causes of Sin need to be
rendered not to create sin, but to be sin itself.
To the Leviathan, sin events are but a microcosmic incarnation of their final goal, broadly
known as Returning To Eden. Those who work to further this exodus are hunted down and
eviscerated by a torrent of Blackcoats and SERAPH memory modifications, but there
remains the stain. The knowledge that something could not entirely be removed by dogma
and fear, and simply watches in the wings, standing beyond the veil,for the need of
unquestioning charity and unflinching intervention.
Leviathan’s ‘charity’ efforts are as indiscriminate as they are dangerous. Aiding Binder and
Exorcists alike in tight situations. Of the times this cooperation with CAIN is notable, CAIN
may sacrifice some of its most promising hammers to be stained with the Sin Marks left in
the wake of Leviathan’s own great psychic anomalies known as the ‘Horsemen’, in order to
understand it all the greater.
Using The Horsemen and Leviathan:
This is an optional supplement for Tom Bloom’s ‘CAIN.’ Intended to offer optional
encounters and progression for campaigns. Using a shadowy, whispered legend traded
among working exorcists. Of a power that even CAIN's machinations cannot fully hide. The
elusive Leviathan.
Leviathan is intended, in campaigns, to offer a further layer of threat to certain plot threads
the exorcists may uncover, and to add a layer to the organisation that controls so much of
the players existence - the idea that dissent against CAIN’s bureaucratic measures for the
sake of worldly preservation can be opposed somehow, and that they are aware of this.
Not only aware in fact, but utilising these interactions to sacrifice their own members for
an experimental tempering against the continual threat of sin, and sin events.
Leviathan, and the Four Horsemen, have no full parallels, but ways in which they function
can be inspired by the following, for ideas for interpretations for play:
The Darkness from ‘The Darkness’ (Video Game, 2007)
Wintermute from ‘Neuromancer’ (Novel, 1984)
The Incubators (Puella Magi Madoka Magica, Anime Series/Franchise, 2011)
The Bene Tleilax from ‘Dune’ (Book series, 1969-1985)
The Moon Presence / The Hunters from ‘Bloodborne’ (Video game, 2015)
The Thousand Sons from ‘Warhammer 40k’ (Tabletop game)
The Patriots from the ‘Metal Gear’ series (1987-2015)
There will be examples given of how certain aspects of Leviathan may function, and how
The Horsemen, and their Disciples may appear as characters. These are presented as
examples, and you may change about whatever features you desire. Leviathan has many
forms, never just the ones known or described.

The Wrath, The Weight and The Lashes:
Whenever you choose to bond with an Original Sin, you gain two new
agenda points marked as ‘The Wrath.’ Whenever you complete a mission
while in possession of a bond with an Original Sin you gain a Lash. Each
lash you have contributes to the overall Weight of your bond, and the
potency of your control over the Original Sin you have access to.
The punishments for failing to act in accordance with your Wrath deal
often with amounts of gained Sin. While sin overflow may still occur to
bonded exorcists, they may instead choose to lose a mark of their
Mortality, loosing a mark to completely erase all sin they have, returning to
0. Each character with a bond to an original Sin gains six overall marks of
their Mortality, after six marks, they suffer an agonising psychic rendering
that over the course of a week, one that over seven days completely
removes them from reality.

‘The Choir’: Original Sin of the Lion//Lamb.
Witness
CAIN records prescribes the source ability of the Original Sin known as ‘The
Choir’ to be part of the Lion//Lamb itself. No source is truly able to be pinned
down as the benefactor of the ability, nor where the commands of all Horsemen
originate from, leading TEMERITY research to conclude three main theories of
origin.
The Choir is itself the Lion//Lamb, a formless psychic presence that bleeds
across grace like poison, and uses Sin Seeds as nooks for its drilling grasp. The
Choir is a manifestation of the Lion//Lamb’s will. Gestating its abilities into the
power to form idle, otherwise mindless servants. The Choir is a gestalt semi-
consciousness, a tumour amidst the psychic veil that manifests in the creation
of the graceless, ‘blank’ constructs known as SALT.
Individuals who manifest a connection to the Choir are cursed with the weight
of ‘Empathy’, the physiological and psychic mimicry of a pale, long, stoic figure
known as ‘Moros’, the solemn embodiment of doom.
No disciples, or encounters with the sourceless origin of the Choir itself
have gleaned any possibility of an anchoring ‘Horsemen’, meaning its point of
contact is simply a byproduct of the presence of other Disciples. If each
Horsemen is the pillar that holds Leviathan up, and the Disciples are the blood
flowing in between, then the Choir is the sound of their very existence, walking
under the watch of the Lion and the Lamb.

Exorcists [x] and [x] sent for reconditioning. A routine clean-up job in [x], Russia,
following the execution of a CAT 2 Ogre. After readings had ceased, mid-
operation wetwork crews were assaulted by a group of ‘blank’, humanoid figures.
Dressing and operating as if they were specialist private militia, the combatants

but instead piles of ‘salt-like’ white powder in the place of the opponent’s
corpses and gore. No known, or identifiable, chemical composition upon
TEMERITY research.
Sin Scar: (d6) The choir coats the throat, opening abscess of darkness and
pustules of shadow. The tongue slaps against the marble of the church, the cold
reflective sights of the tenement halls mirrored in its spit.
1: Once a mission, you can speak a single word to d6 humans in the same room
as you. Whether a general command or emotion, so long as it doesn’t physically
harm the humans, they are struck with an urge to comply with it for one minute.
The Choir makes your voice echo, as if spoken in ceaseless, cavernous
darkness.
2: Regular humans fear to hear your voice in reprimand. They will be more likely
to try to help you, regardless of what limited ways in which they can, if only out
of fear. Accents of your voice are tinged with sinister, incomprehensible groans.
3: When you focus, the immediate thoughts of humans whisper themselves to
you, often only a few words, if ever more than one. Aspects of your voice sound
familiar, coloured with the bliss of nostalgia.
4: When you focus, you subtly implant a believable, albeit likely undesired,
thought into the head of a willing exorcist, or any unwilling human within a short
distance. This may be enough to briefly aid an exorcist, or throw off a human.
Your voice is sweet and thick, coated with phlegm and honey. Rumbling with a
struggled malice.
5: Your voice can softly influence the waking world. For example if a
mechanism that is keeping you from somewhere is easily unlocked, but you
don't have the time, you can speak, and it shall open. Likewise for locking,
jamming, un-jamming, helping move and otherwise influencing objects of a size
scaling with your CAT to a maximum of CAT-4.
6: When rolling for a skill that involves a dialogue, you may add +1D to the
combined roll. Your voice refuses silence, and is always just the loudest in the
room.


Original Sin: The Choir
Summon, Adjacent, Until Rest.
A venomous whisper that causes the host of the Choir’s sinseed to rupture, softly
bleeding into the surrounding tissue. Allowing the host to create mimic-like
‘Murmurs’, psychic reflections of the dead or forgotten. Without proper training,
and permissions from The Choir, use of this Original Sin leads to the slow
‘blanking’ of the user’s own physical, and mental, characteristics.
Max out your Sin. (Minimum of 1 Blank Mark.)
Until you rest, create a Murmur in an adjacent space next to you. You create a
number of Murmurs equal to your CAT score. They share the abilities and roughly
look similar to Security, Mercenaries, or Illuminati. At Lash Severity 2 you may
instead make one Drifter, but only for the remainder of the scene.
Human Murmurs often look like figures the exorcist has killed, or is aware have
died, and typically are unable to talk or react to anything above the fulfillment of
their given commands. When their duration times out, or they are destroyed, they
melt into a coarse, white substance known as SALT. When rubbed on mundane
weapons, SALT makes a single attack with the weapon equal to the user’s CAT.
Though this can only be used on a weapon once, before it too becomes SALT.

II: When you use The Choir, you may choose to generate more types of SALT
murmurs.
III: You can safely use The Choir twice per mission. The second time does not
affect your total Sin.

The Great White Crown: Original sin of The Horseman of Conquest.
Zelos.
Judge
CAIN records prescribes the source ability of the Original Sin known as the
‘Great White Crown’ to be the disciple's version of the powers exercised by
the Horseman known as ‘Conquest’ or ‘Zelos.’ In the rare occurrence of an
encounter with a Horseman Zelos is noted, in the few records that are
made by survivors, to cause a painful, traumatic invocation of an
unprecedented rise in CAT scores, the related power and the effects of
psychic phenomena. Of the few reported manifestations of the Horsemen,
psychic phenomena in the wake of Conquest is recorded to be notably
unique, erratic, and otherwise creating a large number of potential
candidates for CAIN’s exorcist programme. SEER operatives have labelled
these highly monitored, officially non-existent zones of continual influence
as ‘Harvest’ zones, labelled from Alpha to Iota. Though only 2 of these
zones have been credibly marked as areas where Conquest was sighted.
Individuals who manifest a connection to Conquest are cursed with the
weight of ‘Guilt’, the physiological and psychic mimicry of a wizened,
brooding, quiet figure known as ‘Midas’, the regal embodiment of loathing.
Where the Choir is the sound of the temple, and the disciples are the
blood of its flowing pillars, the Horseman Zelos dances in the halls as the
first call of Judgement. The jury of the choir witnesses its final words in the
wake of the shadow of the crown.
Documented encounter with Conquest’s ‘The Great White Crown’:
Exorcists [x], [x], [x], [x] and [x] collected post-mortem for further
investigative research. The exorcists were part of a CAT-4 research
deployment in another potential area for labelling as a ‘Harvest’ zone.
Assumed, but not documented, exposure to Horseman Zelos resulted in the
exorcists, in a mere seconds according to readings, ballooning to CAT-7, and
then metastasising into Sins. Readings in line with a Sin Event quelled by the
13th second of contact. Exorcists returned to original forms, dead. Extensive
combing of the area by the Heaven Eyes and Virtues turned up no further
disturbances.
Sin Scar: (d6) The crown moulds the skull, rippling the skin and
forming a great, marrow-filled ornament of regality. The heavy head that
watches the world pass by, uttering its royal decree amidst folded fingers,
and plotting puppetwork.
1: You may make brief, fleeting alterations to your facial structure. Enough
to pass quickly as someone else, or to confuse security recognition, the
modifications revert after a few minutes. The crown is soft, stringy with
parting phalanges softly rippling underneath pliable skin.
2: There is a latent authority that humans sense you have, maybe you
seem officious, or maybe they simply recognise the crown permeating
through the psychic veil. The crown is spiked, thick ridges adorned with
sharp, painful-looking points that threaten to break the skin.
3: The next immediate goals and motives of humans within the same room
as you can be sensed by you should you will it so, though these come
across as vague, clouded imagery rather than anything substantial. The
crown is hollow in places, divots giving way to eyes that softly roll
underneath the thin skin of the head.
4: You can choose a human within short range, and implant the vaguest
sense of a desire that they can quickly fulfil. This is not immediately
distracting, but implants a latent thing that you may be able to fulfil, or talk
about. The crown curves forward, thick arches and pyramid formations
point forward from the face.
5: Whispers that cannot be quelled make their way into your head mere
moments before an alarm is raised, or your safety is compromised. Should
you act on the aid you take 1 stress, but +1D on your next roll. The crown is
thick, and reaches down to cover your eyes, leaving them small, and
shaded from the light of misguidance.
6: When rolling for a skill that involves making an impression, you may add
+1D to the combined roll. The crown is regal and remarkable, adorning the
peaks of your head.


Original Sin: The Great White Crown
Transmute, Short, Instant.
An insidious scar that causes the host of the Crown’s sinseed to harden, and
show itself. Pushing itself to the surface of the skin, boring through bone and
muscle. Allowing the host to momentarily influence the power of other psychic
phenomena, but leaving themselves exposed. Without proper training, and
permissions from The Choir, use of this Original Sin leads to progressive
deterioration of the host’s body, and the maddening of their grip upon the
psychic veil.
Max out your Sin. (Minimum of 1 Blank Mark.)
Choose one exorcist, sin or human within a short distance from you. You can:
Influence the CAT by a range equal to [CAT-1] to [Your CAT+2] of their next
attack, psychic power or overall abilities, if they are an exorcist
If they are a Sin, until the Sin’s next reaction, you may decrease its total CAT by
2, to a minimum of 1.
If they are a human, momentarily give them a CAT score equal to your own, if
human. Though afterwards they must roll a d6. On a 5-6 they survive, barely. On
a 4 or below they violently explode.
Then, take an injury, and gain +1 scrip.

Curse, Long, 1 Scene.

sharpen itself inside the body. Lacerating and grazing itself upon the surroundings in

other creatures linked to the psychic veil, while throwing themselves into a stupor.
Without proper training, and permissions from The Choir, use of this Original Sin leads

The Everblack Scales: Original sin of The Horseman of Famine. Limos.
Sentencer
CAIN records prescribes the source ability of the Original Sin known as the
‘Everblack Scales’ to be the disciple’s version of the powers exercised by the
Horseman known as ‘Famine’ or ‘Limos.’ In the rare occurrence of an encounter
with a Horseman, Limos is regarded as the most actively encountered
Horseman, appearing to incite sudden world events and drive large swathes of
seemingly innocent humans, and exorcists, to belligerent, hallucinatory
madness. Of the few reported manifestations of the Horsemen, Famine lacks
the numbers in terms of residual psychic traces left on nearby lifeforms,
however the indelible scars left on the world at large leaves Famine as the most
remarkable Horseman in terms of the irreversible changes it can bring.
