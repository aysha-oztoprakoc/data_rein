Introduction
Cloaks can sometimes be magical items in role playing 
games, though one that is perhaps not as common as oth-
er items. Some may have hoods, others may not and they 
can come in different designs and colours.
This supplement has a sample with six different cloaks; 
the full supplement will have 36 magical cloaks suitable 
for fantasy role playing games, especially those that could 
be considered OSR, but also other d20 systems. There are 
some generalised references to stats, which will work for 
many systems.
These can be used by a Game Master as magical treasure 
for characters to find, or items for NPCs to use. The cloaks 
vary in how useful they are, and some may even be dan-
gerous to the wearer. The GM could also use them as mag-
ical robes or capes.

1
Animate Cloak: The animate cloak almost ap-
pears to possess some kind of sentience, but 
doesn’t register as any kind of intelligent magi-
cal item. It frequently moves of its own accord, 
although it never interferes with or gets in the 
way of its wearer. The animate cloak is resistant 
to damage and is capable of deflecting missiles 
fired at the wearer, primarily from the back, and 
can also entangle attackers, like the spell, who 
are within melee weapon range. The ability of the 
animate cloak to intercept attacks from the rear 
make any attempt to surprise the wearer from any 
direction be at -3.
2
Boat Cloak: The boat cloak looks like an ordinary 
brown cloak, though one with a strange pattern 
that is somewhat reminiscent of wooden planks. 
The boat cloak can be activated once per day, and 
will remain activated until deactivated. When the 
boat cloak is removed and the command word 
spoken, it will transform into a small boat, one 
about the size of a coracle and only large enough 
for a single person. The boat doesn’t have any 
oars, instead being powered by magic, and is 
steered the same way
3
Camper’s Cloak: The camper’s cloak will, when 
activated, unfurl to make a campsite, one consist-
ing of a tent big enough for two people as well as 
a magical campfire complete with cooking utensils. 
The campsite will be protected by the equivalent 
of an alarm spell in a ring 40’ in diameter centred 
on the campfire. Anything crossing that, visible or 
invisible, will trigger an alarm. The campsite, once 
activated, will remain in place for 8 hours, unless 
ended sooner, at which point it will turn back into 
a cloak. The camper’s cloak can be activated once 
per day.

Cloak of Absorption: The cloak of absorption is 
intended to absorb spells targeted at the wear-
er. It will only absorb spells that are both ranged 
attacks and specifically targeted at the wearer; 
area-effect spells, such as fireballs, will not be 
stopped. The cloak has a base chance of 30% of 
absorbing a spell, reduced by 5% for every lev-
el of the spell, and spells of a high enough level 
to reduce this to zero will not be absorbed. The 
cloak of absorption can absorb up to 20 spell lev-
els before needing to discharge these, which can 
be done as a targeted energy attack on something 
up to 30’ away, doing 1 point of damage per ab-
sorbed level, save for half. The energy can be dis-
charged harmlessly if there isn’t a desired target, 
and the cloak will not work again until the surplus 
energy is discharged.

Cloak of Blending: The cloak of blending is a 
drab brown cloak with nothing to make it stand 
out at all; not the fabric, not the manufacture 
and not the material. In every way, the cloak 
appears to be completely normal and unmemo-
rable. This property is conferred upon the wearer 
of the cloak; though they don’t become invisible 
or disappear from sight, they become completely 
unmemorable too, blending with the background. 
This makes the wearer hard to notice unless 
someone is concentrating, which requires a Wis-
dom check every round, and there is a +2 bonus 
to any stealth-related checks when the cloak of 
blending is worn.
6
Cloak of Dapperness: The cloak of dapperness 
is an elegantly woven cloak that is clearly of fine 
and expensive manufacture, yet also designed 
with exceptionally good taste so that it looks good 
rather than tackily expensive. The cloak of dap-
perness will alter its form, colours and pattern 
so that it complements the wearer and whatever 
other clothes they are currently wearing; it always 
looks good, no matter who wears it and what they 
are wearing. The wearer gains a +2 bonus to their 
Charisma score whilst wearing the cloak and any 
Charisma-related tests are done with a +2 bonus.
