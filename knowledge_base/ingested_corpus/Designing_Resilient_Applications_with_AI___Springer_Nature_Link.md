Abstract
This chapter examines the principles and practices for designing resilient applications that
leverage artificial intelligence to maintain operational continuity under adverse conditions.
Resilience in modern software systems encompasses the ability to anticipate, withstand,
recover from, and adapt to disruptions ranging from component failures to sophisticated
cyber-attacks. This chapter establishes resilience-first architecture principles that embed
fault tolerance, redundancy, and adaptability into system foundations. Fault tolerance
mechanisms and graceful degradation strategies are explored, demonstrating how AI
enables intelligent failure handling that maintains essential functionality. Self-healing
systems powered by machine learning algorithms detect anomalies, diagnose root causes,
and execute automated remediation without human intervention. Adaptive defense
