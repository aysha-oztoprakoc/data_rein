Quick Reference Guide for GMs and Players
MAKING SKILL ROLLS IN OMEGA HORIZON
[Roll] Skill Group & [Keep] Trait = Base Roll Result 
[Add] Skill Specialty Ranks and any Modifiers
(positive and/or negative) = Final Roll Result
Rolling a six on three or more dice as part of a single Skill 
or Trait roll results in Critical Success. Rolling a Critical 
Success provides the following benefits:
	» The Skill roll automatically succeeds—no matter the 
TN—as long as the character has any reasonable 
way to accomplish the action they are attempting.
	» The character whose player rolled the Critical 
Success gains the Inspired Condition (see Combat 
Conditions).
TOKEN OF DESTINY FACETS
Facet 1 “Close Enough”: Automatically succeed on 
a failed non-combat Skill roll as though you had hit the 
TN exactly.
Facet 2 “Anything Worth Doing…”: Turn a success­
ful non-combat Skill roll into a Critical Success.
Facet 3 “Make It Count”: Take a failed combat Skill 
roll (attack roll) and turn it into a Critical Success to ig­
nore your target’s DR and deal an additional 1k1 dam­
age on the attack.
Facet 4 “Not Today!”: Negate all damage received 
by the player’s character from one source for the Round. 
This can be an environmental source such as a raging fire 
or falling from a great distance, a specific weapon such 
as a bomb, or all attacks made against them by a specific 
character for the entire Round.
Facet 5 “I Know A Guy”: Nudge the GM to throw 
your character a bone story-wise, providing a hint as to 
what might be the best way to handle whatever challenge 
you are facing or move the story forward in a way that 
directly ties into your character’s backstory.
COMBAT ACTIONS
Draw/Holster Weapon: 1 Action Point. Allows a char­
acter to draw or holster a weapon, or to retrieve an item 
from a belt or bag.
Burst Fire Attack: 3 Action Points. Used to make a spe­
cial attack with ranged weapons that are specifically Burst 
Fire Capable. Allows the attacker to hit multiple enemies 
with a single attack, or to hit a single enemy with more 
bullets. The attacking character takes a -3 penalty to their 
Ranged Weapons Skill roll, plus an additional -2 penalty 
for each target included beyond the first. However, if 
successful, the attack deals an additional 1k1 damage 
to each target struck. Burst Fire Attack uses two Shots of 
ammunition for the ranged weapon used for the attack.
Move: 1 Action Point. Allows a character to move up 
to their Base Movement Speed. This movement can be 
broken up so that some of the distance can be moved 
before or after an attack or other action.
Reload: 1 Action Point. Used when a character has fired 
all the Shots in a ranged weapon to reload it, restoring its 
full number of Shots.
Standard Attack: 2 Action Points. Make an attack 
against an enemy using the appropriate Skill roll, typical­
ly Melee Weapons or Ranged Weapons. When making 
a Standard Attack with a ranged weapon, one Shot of 
ammunition is used.
Tumble: 1 Action Point. The character makes a Tumble 
Skill roll (associated with the Athletics Skill Group) vs. TN 
20 (or TN 15 if they possess the Acrobat Advantage). If 
successful, the character halves all incoming BP damage 
until the start of their next Turn in combat. This action can 
be used Reflexively, if the character is about to take dam­
age from an impending attack or environmental effect.
Two Weapon Fighting: 3 Action Points. When wield­
ing two or more one-handed melee weapons, this action 
allows them to make two Standard Attacks as part of a 
single action. The attacker can choose to use both attacks 
against the same target, or against different targets if they 
are both within range, but must use a separate weapon 
for each of the two attacks.

COMBAT CONDITIONS
Blinded (can be applied by Tear Gas Grenades)
Targets with this Condition suffer a -10 penalty to Vision 
Skill rolls and a -5 penalty to all other Skill rolls (including 
attack rolls). Additionally, Blinded characters gain the 
Partial Surprise Condition (see below).
Grievous Wounds (can be applied by Sniper Rifle)
Targets with this Condition bleed for 1 BP damage per hour 
until Triage is performed and only heal 1 BP per day unless 
treated with Long-Term Care. Additional attacks made against 
the target inflict an additional 1 BP worth of bleed damage that 
is not reduced by DR or affected by Juggernaut or Tumble.
Inspired (gained from Critical Success rolls)
The target receives a +1 bonus to the result of all Skill rolls and 
Reflexive Raw Trait rolls. Additionally, the target can re-roll one 
failed Reflexive Raw Trait roll, resolving this Condition early.
Rooted (can be applied by Net Shooter)
No movement-related actions can be taken by the target, 
such as the Move or Tumble actions.
Partial Surprise
Applies to characters who are aware they are in danger (such 
as from active combat situations), but who are not directly 
aware of a specific attacker or their current location. Reduces 
the character’s Physical Defense TN by three, and successful 
attacks against them deal an additional 1k1 damage.
Full Surprise
Only applies to characters who are completely unaware 
they are about to enter combat. Sets the character’s total 
Physical Defense TN to 5, and successful attacks against 
them deal an additional 2k2 damage.
SPECIES ATTRIBUTES
Humans
Human Ingenuity: Once per game session, the char­
acter can call upon this ability to swap in a completely 
unrelated Skill Specialty they have more ranks in when 
attempting a Skill roll, coming up with a clever or inven­
tive solution using outside-the-box thinking.
Unyielding Resolve: When reduced to zero BP or less, 
the character may make a Reflexive Raw Constitution roll 
(TN 18) to remain conscious.
Rigellians
Children of Rigel: Provides various Resistances includ­
ing natural DR 2, Fire Resist 3, and Radiation Resist 15.
Vomeronasal Sense: By tasting the air, rigellians can 
identify familiar individuals’ scents and can even decipher 
what surrounding creatures may be feeling. Rigellians 
register this heightened sense of taste as a mix of smell 
and sight—colors in the air—providing a +3 modifier to the 
result of all Vision and Empathy Skill rolls. This also allows 
rigellians to “see” in dim light and no-light conditions.
Yuanji
Engineered for Battle: Provides natural DR 3 and an 
additional 2 Action Points to use each Round in combat.
Rapid Regeneration: Triples the amount of BP healed 
after taking a Full Night’s Rest.
ADVANTAGES & DISADVANTAGES
Acrobat: Lowers the Target Number (TN) for Tumble 
Skill rolls from 20 to 15 and provides a +2 Modifier to 
Balance and Tumble Skill rolls.
Action Surge: Provides two additional Action Points 
which a player can use once per combat Encounter.
Artful Dodger: Increases the result of Deceit, Forgery, 
and Sleight of Hand Skill rolls.
Athlete: Increases the result of Climb, Jump, and Swim 
Skill rolls.
Danger Sense: Prevents a character from being subject 
to Full Surprise (see Combat Conditions) and provides a +3 
modifier to Perception Skill rolls to sense impending danger.
Juggernaut: Passively halves all incoming physical dam­
age after Resistances (including DR) are applied.
Pilot Certification: Eliminates the +5 TN penalty for 
flying starships of a specified class. For example, Pilot 
Certification for Class III starships include light freighters 
like the Lone Justice.
Rapid Reload: Allows the character to reload a ranged 
weapon as a Free Action (zero Action Point cost).
Rotten Luck: Creates the possibility of a Critical Failure 
that occurs if a character rolls three or more 1s on a Skill or 
Trait roll. Critical Failure supersedes Critical Success.
Slippery: Increases the result of Disable Device, Escape 
Artist, and Larceny Skill rolls.
Whirling Blades: When using the Two Weapon Fighting 
action in combat, a character with this Advantage always 
treats their target as having the Partial Surprise Condition 
(see Combat Conditions).
GENETIC MODS & TECH AUGMENTS
Note that any Trait level increases or other secondary 
attributes like DR increases are already factored into the 
characters’ stat blocks
Bionic Arms: Increases Strength Trait level.
Carbon Steel Muscle Fibers: Increases Strength Trait 
level and provides natural DR.
Cheetah DNA: Provides one additional Action Point per 
Round in combat and increases Initiative roll.
Ocular Implant: Allows the character to see in different 
spectrums (heat vision, night vision, etc.) and provides +3 
modifier to Vision Skill rolls.
Neural Link: Implant that powers and allows a charac­
ter to control any tech augments they may possess.
