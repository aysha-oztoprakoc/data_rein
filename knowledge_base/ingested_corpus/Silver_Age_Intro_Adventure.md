There Is a Fountain 
Wednesday, February 6, 1991: ​
Last Waning Crescent of the Lost Highway Moon 
 
Your Shadow is 4. Your Moonlight 1. It’s 47 
Fahrenheit at 1:30 AM. Skye Reilly is missing. 
 
Skye turned 18 last week. A lone wolf who found 
herself down in West Texas Juvenile Detention 
center last year, she’s wanted to join your pack since 
she got out. She’s been living 60 miles north of your 
territory around Hattie, up in Sweetwater, making 
peace with her mom, but around midnight her mom 
called you and said Skye’d taken a night bus down 
here and she wanted to make sure she got there 
safe. You know the bus got to the Greyhound stop 
around 11 PM, and Beverly Reeder at Jaunita’s Cafe 
saw Skye trying to use the pay phone before 
someone — it looked like Pastor Franklin Bradley of 
the Apostolic Assembly — offered her a ride. She 
knows him because her grandson Jake’s been going 
there. 
 
There aren’t many places to go in Hattie, even fewer 
this time of night. If she’s not at The Silver Palace 
(and she’s not) and not at Juanita’s All-Night Cafe 
(and she’s not), she’s probably in trouble. Besides 
Jaunita’s and The Silver Palace, the only place open 
on a Wednesday night in Hattie is the all-night 
prayer vigil at Apostolic Assembly. 
 
Since the waning quarter moon, the pack in Hattie 
has known there were two additional wolves living 
in Hattie besides the pack and the lone wolves they 
know about, and they know they can’t identify who 
or where those wolves are, other than they aren’t in 
the prison nor in The Silver Palace. 
 
What’s Really Going On. Most of Apostolic 
Assembly’s congregation came out tonight to 
“spend a night in prayer for the town of Hattie and 
its people.” Only some in his small but devout 
congregation know what Pastor Bradley, a former 
Department of Defense biochemist, is “converting” 
werewolves into near-mindless warriors for God. 
Skye is the third lone werewolf he’s kidnapped, 
figuring she won’t be missed. She’ll join the two 
AWOL airmen from Dyess AFB in Abilene that he’s 
turned into Big Blues, werewolves overloaded with 
exposure to silver. He’s gradually immersing her 
with silver sulfate, warping her into a permanent 
hybrid, lonely soul leeched away, skin and fur turned 
blue from the silver sulfate. The Air Force planned 
to quietly turn Big Blues their new special 
weapons—airmen whose families think they’re 
already dead and who can drop into enemy 
territories and survive long enough to cause 
destruction. Bradley stole the plans and the 
technology to build his only little righteous 


 
dominion here in West Texas. He doesn’t know 
about your pack or any of your packmates, but has 
discovered several of the lone wolves who used to 
be in West Texas Juvenile Detention Center. 
 
Apostolic Assembly of Hattie 
Apostolic Assembly opened in 1989 and still 
worships in a converted two-story home built in the 
1920s. Affiliated with the fast-growing Apostolic 
Assembly movement, which has burgeoning 
congregations in other small cities and towns 
across the south and southwest, Hattie’s Apostolic 
Assembly has stayed small and insular, drawing an 
odd combination of retired Air Force officers, Callie 
County Sheriff’s deputies, and some local 
ne’er-do-wells. 
 
Only Franklin Bradley, Eddie Brand, Vince 
Hammond, and Cornelia Saunders know about 
werewolves and what is going on upstairs. Jake 
Reeder knows Skye is upstairs, but not that she’s 
drugged or in danger. Those marked with the an 
asterisk use the Hunter statblock. The rest use 
Congregant statblock. 
Outside the Church 
The old driveway has space for just two cars: Pastor 
Franklin’s brown Oldsmobile and Vince’s black 
Camaro. Everyone else is parked on the street. 
There are few lights on in most of the houses 
around the church, so its likely most of the 
neighbors are asleep. The yard is trimmed but with 
few shrubs and no trees. 
At the Vigil 
When the characters arrive, it’s the vigil has already 
been going on for a few hours, though several 
attendees have come and gone throughout the 
evening, including the pastor who is currently 
upstairs in his study. 
The Vigil Downstairs 
Most of the walls in the downstairs area have been 
taken out and replaced with support beams, leaving 
the entrance and social area open to the kitchen 
and worship area. Most of the attendees are sitting 
quietly, with several demonstrably kneeling and 
praying, while listening to Vince play praise songs 
on the guitar. Ever the politician, Chance Ransom, 
who isn’t a member of the congregation, is the most 
likely to spot and greet newcomers, though he’ll 
speak in hushed tones. There’s scorched coffee and 
some Girl Scout cookies in the kitchen, as well as 
Francine James’ homemade bourbon fruitcake. 
 
To the right of the main door, there are staircases 
up to the second floor from the kitchen and the 
social room. Between the staircases, with a small 
door from the side of the house, is a mudroom. 
There is a small washroom under the rear stair. 
 
Werewolves can smell “tainted” sulfurous silver 
(actually Silver Sulfate) throughout the house and 
hear the sound of running water from upstairs. If 
they try to go upstairs, Cornelia and Eddie will try to 
firmly but gently discourage them. 
People 
 
Eddie Brand (He/Him, 23, sheriff’s deputy). The 
youngest deputy in Callie County, Brand is uptight 
and self-righteous. He knows about, but doesn’t 
understand, Pastor Bradley’s kidnapped 
werewolves, and assumes that the “conversions” 
will make Hattie and the world safer. He’s seen the 
hybrids upstairs, but they still scare him, even if he 
won’t admit it. 
 
Rick Garcia (He/Him, 24, sheriff’s deputy). Local 
part-boy turned sheriff’s deputy, he figures 
Apostolic Assembly is a good place to kiss up to his 
colleagues and bosses. He’s known Vince most of 
his life and has never trusted him. He has no idea 
what’s really going on. 
 
Vince Hammond (He/Him, 25, youth pastor and 
local thug)*. Vince publicly represented from his life 
of sin and depravity last summer, just before Pastor 
Bradley appointed him as Apostolic Assembly’s 


 
youth pastor. His ways don’t seem to have changed, 
though: He still spends most of his time playing with 
his band at The Silver Palace or up at dance halls in 
Abilene or selling illegal guns to hunters. 
 
Francine James (She/Her, 64, retired librarian). 
When Hattie reduced library staff last year, 
Francine, the former head librarian, retired. She 
doesn’t really like Apostolic Assembly, but since her 
surgery last month, it’s easier to walk here from her 
house down the block than to get across Main 
Street to Hattie Community Church. 
 
Gloria Jaurez (She/Her, 32, bartender). A new 
day-shift bartender at The Silver Palace, she’s been 
seeing Vince Hammond for a few weeks, and 
hanging around church when he’s here. She’s 
actually a reporter from The Dallas Times-Herald, 
investigating the suppliers who sell Vince guns. 
 
Cliff Layton (He/Him, 22, teacher). Recently moved 
from San Antonio, the new high school social 
studies teacher started coming to Apostolic 
Assembly a few weeks ago, never really admitting 
to himself it was because of his attraction to Vince 
Hammond. 
 
Chance Ransom (He/Him, 49, Callie County 
Sheriff). No one expected Sheriff Ransom, a 
Methodist and conservative democrat living in 
Twain City, to show up at midnight for a prayer vigil 
20 miles east of his home. With an upcoming 
special election for the state senate, he’s hoping to 
bolster his appeal to conservative Christians—and 
his own deputies—in the senate district. 
 
Jake Reeder (He/HIm, 18, busboy). Jake is Beverly 
Reeder’s grandson, and the late Jaunita’s son, and 
busboy at Jaunita’s Cafe. Since doing his own short 
stint in West Texas Juvenile Detention, he’s cut his 
hair and been attending Apostolic Assembly. He 
knows Skye is upstairs, but is unaware she’s been 
drugged or held against her will. He openly admires 
Frank and Vince. 
 
Hannah Samson (She/Her, 32, Pastor of Hattie 
Community Church). Hoping to smooth over 
differences between the Hattie’s two small 
congregations, Hannah decided to make an 
appearance at Apostolic Assembly’s prayer vigil. 
She’s barely hiding her discomfort with this 
congregation’s misogyny and is planning to leave. 
 
Cornelia Saunders (She/Her, 55, retired sheriff’s 
deputy)*. The first woman deputy in Callie County 
retired in 1989 and started attending Apostolic 
Assembly. Before then, she’d had an on-again, 
off-again relationship with Beverly Reeder. She’s 
Pastor Bradley’s confidante in the werewolf 
conversion program. 
 
Lisa Torres (She/Her, 29, juvenile detention 
guard). Lisa lives close to the edge of Porterhead 
Trail State Park. Until her boyfriend left, she ran a 
preschool out of her home, then she got a job as a 
guard at West Texas Juveniel Detention. She’s been 
coming to Apostolic Assembly since November. If 
her relationship with Eddie Brand is supposed to be 
a secret, it’s a poorly kept one. 
Upstairs from the Vigil 
The sound of music comes from 
downstairs—unless the characters have pushed 
past Eddie and Cordelia, in which case Vince will join 
Eddie and Cordelia in trying to get the werewolves 
to leave. Eddie, Cordelia, and Vince won’t like it, but 
Hannah Samson and Chance Ransom will try to 
“help” calm everyone down. 
 
Bradley’s first two silver ghouls, Ray and Lilly, are 
here. Rooms except for these three are empty, even 
dusty. 
 
Room A. This bedroom is converted to Frank 
Bradley’s study. He’s here, saying a prayer over a 
container of silver sulfate solution. He will come out 
if there’s commotion on the upper floor or if anyone 
calls out to him. He’s armed with a semiautomatic 
handgun. His books include a number of bound 
classified reports on something called Project Big 
Blue. 
 
Room B. Skye is here in this small bathroom, 
sedated in the tub, wearing shorts and a t-shirt. The 
water is running through a pump, gradually adding 
increasing amounts of silver sulfate into the water. 
Her body is slowly starting to shift to Hybrid form, 
but she’s still primarily human. The pumping device 
and vials of silver sulfate are marked as property of 
the The US Air Force and Goodfellow Air Force 


 
Base. The pumping mechanism says it is classified 
equipment for “Project Big Blue.” 
 
Room C. Airmen Herrara and Sanchez are here, just 
inside the darkened room. The door is slightly ajar 
so they can monitor the hallway, and they are under 
orders to stop anyone besides Frank or Cornelia 
from entering the bathroom but to otherwise stay 
out of sight. 
People and Werewolves 
Franklin “Pastor Frank” Bradley (He/Him, 48, 
Pastor of Apostolic Assembly of Hattie)*. Pastor 
Frank is a biochemist who also teaches science at 
Hattie Unified School and West Texas Juvenile 
Detention. He’s never studied religion or theology, 
but left the Department of Defense when he 
became a pastor. 
 
Ray Herrera (He/Him, 21, AWOL Airman/Silver 
Ghoul). Ray was returning to Goodfellow AFB in San 
Angelo where he worked as a medic from partying 
with some gay friends in Fort Worth when Frank 
and Vince kidnapped him two weeks ago. He is now  
one of their two “Big Blues,” a silver sulfate ghoul. 
 
Skye Reilly (18, She/Her, Lone Wolf): Skye’s the 
missing wolf. She’s just started the silver sulfate 
treatment, but if removed from the solution before 
sunrise, she will recover fully in a few days. Franklin 
Bradley, who taught Skye when she was in Juvenile 
Detention, convinced her to come back to Hattie 
because your pack needed her. 
 
Lily Sanchez (She/Her, 19, AWOL Airman/Silver 
Ghoul).  Lily was returning to Dyess AFB in Abilene 
from a family funeral when Vince and Frank 
kidnapped her. Barely aware of her status as a Lone 
Wolf, she is now a “Big Blue,” the silver sulfate 
ghoul. 
 
Aftermath 
The scientists and tacticians behind the US Air 
Force’s Big Blue Project have already identified 
Frank Bradley as the one who stole the equipment 
and research. They don’t want to move too openly, 
but if they can find an excuse to take over Bradley’s 
work (and any take control of werewolves in Hattie) 
under the guise of “protecting the town,” they will.  
 
If the werewolves drew significant attention in an 
attempt to liberate Skye, Project Big Blue will take 
control of the town under the guise of fighting 
domestic terrorism, displacing the county sheriff’s 
department. If the werewolves were especially 
aggressive, the Air Force “advisors” will be in place 
by the following sunrise. 
 
If the werewolves were subtle, the Project Big Blue 
will simply embed researchers in the town under 
the guise of building a better relationship. 
 
If the pack took control of Airmen Sanchez and 
Herrera, they will have no trouble getting the two 
Silver Ghouls to work for them, but they will have 
trouble hiding their presence in town. It will take 
more than a single lunar month to figure out how, 
where, or who may be able to help the two airmen, if 
it’s even possible. Attempts to divine whether the 
Airmen can be cured will receive the answer “Not 
Yet Known.”
