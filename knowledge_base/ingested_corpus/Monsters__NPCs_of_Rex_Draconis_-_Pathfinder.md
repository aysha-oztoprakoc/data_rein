OFFENSE
Speed 30ft.
Melee wrench +5 (1d6+2) 
Special Attacks Inventions 
Invention formula known (CL 5th)
3rd – tongues, waterwalk 
2nd – fog cloud, levitate, see invisibility 
1st – air bubble, burning hands, featherfall, jump
0th – detect magic, light, mending, spark
STATISTICS
Base Atk +3; CMB +5; CMD 16 
Feats Brew Potion, Craft Wondrous Item, Craft Magic 
Arms and Armor, Extend Spell, Magical Aptitude, 
Toughness.
Skills Craft (alchemy) +13, Craft (simple machines) +13, 
Craft (aircraft) +13, Craft (traps) +13, Disable Device 
+9, Knowledge (engineering) +11, Spellcraft +13, Use 
Magic Device +10 
Languages Common, Dwarven, Draconic, Gnomish.
Equipment – Wrench (treat as mace), Leather apron and 
protective gear (treat as leather armor).
SPECIAL ABILITIES
Elbow Grease (Ex): Esc gains +2 to all Craft checks.
Invention (Ex): Esc has a unique capacity for invention 
and innovation. In addition to any mundane abilities 
of craft, Esc is able to imbue quasi-magical powers 
into his inventions. 
Each device may replicate a single spell ability from 
his known invention formula. Each device requires 
a number of hours and materials equal to 100 gold 
pieces per spell level (ie a 3rd level spell takes 3 hours 
and 300 gold pieces) he may craft a device that can 
generate the spell effect as if he had cast it. Cantrips 
are treated as 1st level spells for the purposes of 
this ability. Anyone may use the created device, but 
the device must adhere to the rules of the spell 
(ie duration, effect etc). Each device may be used a 
number of times equal to his Intelligence modifier 
(3), and then ceases to function unless Esc repairs 
it with a use of his Inventor ability. The device lasts 
for 24 hours, but may be maintained by expending a 
use of this ability.
Devices are fragile. Each device has an AC of 12, and 
hit points equal to twice Esc’s hit dice (10 hp). A 
device losing all of its hit points is destroyed. 
Esc may craft or maintain a number of devices equal 
to 1 plus ½ his class level at any one time (3). The 
materials are not consumed.   
Salvage (Ex): Esc can salvage half the gold piece value 
from any magic item (destroying it in the process) to 
use in creating other items. This process takes one 
day and Esc can only salvage items associated with 
his Crafting Feats.
Example Inventions
Torch – light spell
Parachute – featherfall spell
Flamethrower – burning hands spell
Underwater breather – air bubble spell
Stealthbreaker goggles – see invisibility spell
Universal translator – tongues spell
Nuts and Bolts
* Esc is a highly modified 
version of the Artificer 
class from Adamant 
Entertainment. 
While the class fits 
the character, 
it is unlikely to 
appear in the Rex 
Draconis Campaign 
setting as a playable 
class. Esc is intended 
to remain a unique 
NPC in the world.

The Wheyr
No one is certain as to the origins of the canine 
race, although some claim them an offshoot of 
the ogres. The first reports of wheyr show up 
in old texts from before the Shadowtimes and 
refer to "a party of literal curs out of the chill 
north who laid waste to the Darnathian Empire's 
colony of Bastas".
They have several hidden ports and 
shipbuilding facilities to support their black 
raiders, as their ships are known to outsiders.
Wheyr vary in size, ranging from medium 
to large. Strength matches accordingly. Their 
appearance resembles a bipedal hound, with 
variations based on pack bloodlines.
Leaders are chosen by strength, cunning, 
and savagery. The wheyr are led by a warlord, 
underneath whom are individual pack lords. 
Under the pack lords are alphas, who command 
raiding parties. If an alpha is killed, the second 
in command takes over as alpha.
