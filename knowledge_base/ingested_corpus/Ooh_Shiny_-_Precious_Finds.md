Deluge Band
Aura Moderate conjuration; CL 6th; Slot finger;
Price 9,500 gp; Weight ­.
Icy cold to the touch and constantly wet with
condensation, this pale blue ring leaves little to the
imagination as to what magic lies within it.
DESCRIPTION
The wearer of a Deluge Ring can produce gallons of
crystal­clear, icy cold water at will, much like the create
water spell; however, the water produced by this ring is
so cold that it deals one point of nonlethal cold damage
for each gallon dumped on a creature. A DC 12 Fortitude
save negates. Creatures with the water subtype are
immune to this effect and creatures with the fire subtype
take lethal damage with a ­4 to their saving throw.
CONSTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS
Forge Ring, create water; Cost 3,600 gp, 380 XP.
Jailer’s Shacklering
Aura Moderate conjuration; CL 8th; Slot finger;
Price 14,000 gp; Weight ­.
This plain brass ring has a fine chain about two inches
long hanging off of it.
DESCRIPTION
Used by high profile individuals in the security industry,
such as Captains of the Guard, the wearer of a Jailer’s
Shacklering can swing his arm just so as a standard action
that provokes attacks of opportunity, causing the short
chain attached to the ring to suddenly grow to a
maximum of thirty feet in length. If this chain wraps
around the wrist of an individual, a ranged touch attack,
the end of the chain fuses into a shackle. Once an
individual is shackled, the wearer and his victim then
make
opposed
Strength
checks
as
a
free
action
immediately and on all of the wearer's subsequent turns.
If the wearer wins the Strength check, the wearer elects to
either retract the chain or make it so the victim cannot
use that arm this round. If the victim wins the Strength
check, then the victim can act normally. Further, if the
victim wins the Strength check by 5 or more, he can break
the chain, causing the ring to retract to its standard two
inches.
The Jailer’s Shacklering can be used as many times a day
as the wearer desires; however, should the chain be
broken for any reason, the ring will be unresponsive to
attempts
to
activate
it
for
the
next
24
hours.
CONSTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS
Forge Ring, minor creation; Cost 7,000 gp, 560 XP.
Ring of Excessive Shininess
Aura Moderate enchantment; CL 7th; Slot finger;
Price 6,500 gp; Weight ­.
What squirrels are to dogs, this ring is to everyone else.
DESCRIPTION
The wearer of the Ring of Excessive Shininess can subject
others to the power of true bling, effectively duplicating
the daze monster spell at will. A DC 13 Will save negates.
Creatures dazed by the Ring of Excessive Shininess take
an additional point of precision damage when struck.
CONSTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS
Forge Ring, create water; Cost 3,250 gp, 260 XP.
