This release is a collection of ready-to play cyberpunk player characters for SprawlGoons -
Upgraded. 36 in total, one for each optional background found in the Carbon Edition.
They are meant to be used for quick one-shot sessions, games at conventions and every
other occasion where you could use some pre-gen. 
Most of the content was A.I. generated. Not only the art work, but although the texts, the
generation point distribution, etc. I only edited a few things here and there where it did not
made any sense or was simply to bad to be acceptable. Thereby, you get it all for free.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
To make full use of this supplement, you do need a copy of 
SprawlGoons: Carbon Edition


11. Zone Wars Veteran

Stats: Morphology 2, Reaction 1, Cognition 1
Belongings: Haunt M10 Anti-material Sniper Rifle, 
Heavy Kevlar Jacket, 3 doses Brazilian hyperdex
Edge: Situational Awareness
Dax Voss is a hardened survivor of the global Zone
Wars, having fought in the Mekong Damn Wars, the
African Coast Anti-Piracy Campaign and the Texas
Brushfire Insurgencies. Quitting merc work on a

but scars and ghosts, he now drifts through the Flow,

tactical advisor to local syndicates and fixers who
need someone with experience. He dislikes “grunt
duty”, but takes those jobs, too.

Neurochem (+1 Reaction)
How: Less-than-perfect front line modification he 
got “on the job”.
Drawback: Random combat blackouts — roll on 
Equilibrium rolls in actual combat situations or 
when under heavy stress. On a failure Tom may 
take anybody for an enemy for a scene and forget 
about what happened afterwards.
