4 feet by 6 feet in size. The cavernous sack opens into an

Krampus, Beyond the Veil  
 
As we see in this story, Krampus has a cooperative 
relationship with Santa Claus. While Santa rewards good 
children, Krampus punishes the wicked ones, threatening 
them, beating them, and abducting the worst offenders.  
 
Krampus is arrogant and reckless, impulsive and 
violent. He has little tolerance for wicked children and 
thoroughly enjoys his work. He revels in inflicting terror 
in young people. While he is a formidable combatant, he 
rarely fights opponents that pose a significant threat to 
him. As a result, he tends to be brazen and rash, feeling 
that he has absolutely nothing to fear from children and 
they should have everything to fear from him.  
 
Krampus lives in the Frozen North with Santa and the 
hundreds of elves. Like Santa, he leaves the icy 
northland once every year and travels the globe visiting 
children. But while Santa’s sacks are full of gifts for the 
good children, Krampus’ sacks are empty, ready to be 
filled with wicked children stolen from their beds.  
 
Krampus is judge, jury, and executioner for the most 
devilish children in the world, knowing their secrets and 
meting out punishments as he sees fit. While most are 
beaten and left with a warning, those who pose an 
imminent threat to others or whom he deems incorrigible 
are taken away.  
 
His origins are lost in the many legends and tales that 
have been told about him for generations, but there is no 
doubt that he and Santa are inexorably linked. Some 
have speculated that they are actually two aspects of the 
same spirit, dived into two separate beings.
