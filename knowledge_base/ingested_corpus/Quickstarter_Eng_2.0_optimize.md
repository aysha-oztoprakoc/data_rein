SETTING
      In the Earth Union, the last decade has been a period of unpreceden­
ted prosperity and peace, in which diplomacy and scientific progress 
have replaced barbarism and war. In the wake of this renewed peace, 
science and technology have made an incredible leap forward in a matter 
of years. Almost all known diseases have been eradicated and the quality 
of life has drastically improved; old cars have been replaced worldwide 
by DCS (acronym for domestic class spaceship, or small spaceships 
flying at low altitude); implants and genetic “improvements” are within 
the reach of every budget – although obviously the most incredible tre­
atments are still the prerogative of the rich. The old religions have been 
abandoned in favor of the cult of The One Who Has Many Names – a be­
nevolent god, who preaches peace and concord and has their sanctuary 
in the sciences. The known world, for the first time since monkeys came 
down from the trees, is at peace.
      A real shame that much of what has just been said is nothing more 
than a fragile appearance.
      In reality, humanity is in danger now more than ever, but it is a 
danger lurking in the folds of reality, in the dark corners where ordinary 
people do not look – or perhaps do not want to look. Something dark 
is tightening its grip on humanity, as the news fills up with odd facts 
seemingly unrelated to each other. Men and women who more and more 
often cut ties with friends and relatives for no clear reason, disappearing 
into thin air; accidents without a real explanation, with increasing 
frequency; strange waves of suicides; a world of entertainment and 
video games that seems to try to replace the real world – or to hide it. 
These are actually signs. Signs to which people, blinded by neon lights, 
are not paying attention. The first signs, not simply of war, but of the 
end of the world as humans know it. The Earth, in fact, fell decades ago, 
unbeknownst to most, into a terrible trap that has already imprisoned 
and consumed countless other worlds. Will it be here that the game 
finally ends, or will our world become only the umpteenth shattered 
hope?
      There’s still time.
      There are still tricks up human sleeves.
     But which of the many possible roads is the one that leads to salvation? 
      In AF, players have two roles to play: on the one hand, they play the 
Lost Children, the outcast, the rebels, those who have seen the world 
for what it is and, in exchange for this knowledge, have received eternal 
slavery; on the other hand, they play creatures who have traded in the 
destinies of entire civilizations since before the creation of this world  
- the Aos. In practice, each player will have the sheet of their character 
- a human being tied by a pact called the Exchange to an Aos - and the 
character sheet of another player’s Aos.
      The GM will choose which AOS to assign to which Lost Child.
 Once the pairs have been decided, the Aos will get hold of the original 
names of the characters and decide how the Lost Children will be called 
from now on: this represents the moment in which the Lost Children 
part ways with the human world, of which they will never really be part 
of ever again, if not in appearance.
       To fight the sense of isolation and despair resulting from this - and 
the machinations of Aos, Alchemists, Summoned and so much more - 
the first Lost Children founded over a century ago the Arcana Familia, 
a sort of governmental organization that should bring together all the 
Lost Children active in the world.
      The players will start as newcomers to the Arcana Familia, but what
they will do with their lives will be entirely up to them.
      AF can host chronicles of all types and tastes. A gaming group could 
opt for a campaign set in London’s parlors, where intrigue and power 
games are what the life is all about, intent on trying to survive in a world 
of monsters with smiling faces; another could instead throw itself into 
a series of reckless missions, among ruined buildings and ultra modern 
secret laboratories; yet another group might instead prefer to immerse 
itself totally in the world behind the mirror, trying to discover what is 
really hidden beyond the facade of this wonderful modern world.
      In fact, it is as if the Earth were composed of two different realities - 
the mundane and the mystical-technological - linked together by wires 
and chains of which only a few are aware, and which even fewer are able 
to follow. That said, a chronicle of AF doesn’t necessarily need to pro­
ceed on this double track - indeed, very often the characters will not be 
fully aware of what is really behind the events they are experiencing, but 
maybe they will only catch some bizarre glimpses of the alien world that 
increasingly permeates our own and of the machinations that move it. 
      Their awareness will grow along with their inhumanity and their 
power, but the more they discover the more they will feel that the abyss 
they are looking at is deep - and they will probably begin to wish they 
had never cast that first, fatal glance...
      What all the campaigns of Arcana Familia have in common is the 
relationship between what is human and what is not. The characters 
will find themselves forced to carry on a difficult cohabitation with 
something that perhaps they will never be able to fully understand, 
constantly asking themselves whether, given the possibility, they really 
would like to know what lies behind the existence of the Aos and their 
world, Wonderland. But this is only the first dilemma the Lost Children 
are going to face. With time, in fact, the daily relationship with their Aos 
will no longer be a relationship between human and non-human, but 
between non-human and non-human.

sacrifices of them that they are not willing to make. Putting the matter 
on a less intimate and broader perspective, the dream of the Earth 
Union is a re-proposition on a global scale of the eternal dilemma of the 
characters. Would the idea of a single nation including all the peoples of 
the Earth carried out by Basileo be the real turning point for the human 
race or just the beginning of a new slavery? Wouldn’t a life less comfor­

better? Or maybe this “freedom” would degenerate into an anarchy that 
would actually lead to an unjust society marred by all sorts of conflicts? 
Basileo is not simply a power-hungry tyrant: he is a man who has seen 
the worst of human nature, and is convinced that he has found a way to 
remedy it once and for all. And are the characters really sure that, in the 
end, his is not the right solution?
      Another characteristic of the setting of AF is the deep and complex 
relationship between science and the divine; we are used to a society in 
which, often wrongly or in a simplistic way, we tend to see these two 
concepts as antithetical and in opposition, but it is not so in the world 
of Arcana Familia. This is because it was a real deity, The One Who Has 
Many Names, who taught men a good part of the futuristic technology 
on which the foundations of the world now rest. The same concept of 
thinking machines was little more than a dream relegated to movies 
and novels, before the advent of this creature. And the gods of AF are 
not abstract entities in which to have faith. Many Names has been seen 
emerging into our world; they exist, whether one believes it or not. 
So even religion and the very way of experiencing it have undergone 
a radical change since the years in which man worshiped distant and 
unapproachable divinities.
      Arcana Familia is a game of humans and monsters - but what it means 
to be a human or a monster is up to the players to decide.

birth, and we must learn to make the most of it. If we can accomplish our 
goal, whatever it is and despite the difficulties encountered along the 
way, then we will be able to enjoy the satisfaction of doing what we exist 
for in life, and in death the prize that Many Names reserves for useful

nutshell, is the doctrine preached by the Order of the Royal Art, and the 
one most human beings believe in. Very simple on the surface, perhaps 
it owes its success precisely to its ease of understanding - as well as to 
the marvels that Many Names has given over the years to humans in 
exchange for their devotion, of course. In this world view, the concept

      Yet not everyone is like that.
      There are defective gears, anarchists, rebels. There have always been, 
and there are also in this era of fake peace. The authorities and journali­

composed entirely of broken gears and which very existence is based on 
the rejection of the system. It’s called Arcana Familia.

given by the concept of Many Names’ destiny, they can find themselves 
connected to something they did not even know existed. It is not the 
Internet, even if it seems similar, and its operation involves not only the 
brain but the whole body of the individual. Then, in that place-not place 
that the first rebels called Wonderland, an Aos could decide to make 
contact with this broken gear and grant them, through their own power, 
the ability to change a destiny the future Lost Child has decided they 
could never accept.

were not so, how would people understand the value of what they are 
asking for? To have the power of a god, one must renounce the power 
of another. The rebels demonstrate the purity of their intent and their 
courage by giving their name - the name to which Many Names has 
linked a predetermined fate from birth - to the Aos whose powers they 
desire, and swearing eternal - ETERNAL - loyalty to the creature. This is 
the price for breaking the chains of destiny. It may seem ironic that to

ARCANA FAMILIA THEMES
This game is about the asymmetrical relationship between two 
codependent but extremely different, if not alien, individuals, even if the 
capacity and authority of one is also considerably higher than the other. 
Given that, despite everything, the existence of the first is important or 
fundamental to the other, the game is based on the development of a 
constantly changing and changing balance of the code of conduct and 
manipulations, both positive and coercive, between the two individuals, and 
on the birth of unique and specific social contracts and customs depending 
not only on the power relationship, but also on the approach that both 
individuals present both internally (to each other) and externally (to the rest 
of the world).
The journey that Arcana Familia aims to offer its players is not a mere 
exploration of the fantastic, but also of introspection of interpersonal 
relationships that can become one of respect and coexistence or pain and 
abuse, depending on how they are faced, but still intimate.

EARTH UNION 
      An enlightened monarchy with Basileo as its absolute ruler.
      Next to the Basileo, there is a Senate that legislates and helps the 
Basileo to govern, but in fact the king is the highest authority both 
military and political.
      The term “Basileo” refers to both the proper name of the king and his 
title, since Basileo has been in charge practically since the founding of 
the Union - over a hundred years now. The fact that he has not aged a 
day since his ascent to the throne has made him a sacred figure for the 
people, who now consider him the chosen one of Many Names - a fact 
that gives him enormous powers and incalculable prestige. The Order 
of the Royal Art - the closest thing in the world of AF to an organized 
religion - supports him at the moment, but the support of the Alchemi­
sts grows dimmer every year.
      In fact, the only real limit to the sphere of action of Basileo is the 
need to be careful to not anger the Order of the Royal Art, upon which 
technology the life of his gigantic empire is founded. Multinational 
corporations andbanks have, as in every time and place, a great political 
weight, it is true, but the axis that has been created between science 
and official religion has extremely limited their possibility of exerting 
pressure on the Earth Union. 
      The objective of Earth Union is to bring together the entire planet, 
but until now its efforts have achieved mixed fortunes.
The capital is London.
THE ARCANA FAMILIA
      In short, a secret organization belonging - at least nominally - to the 
army, in charge of gathering, organizing and monitoring all the Lost 
Children and their respective Aos in the world. Officially at the moment 
with “world” they mean the Earth Union, but it is known that Basileo 
uses every possible excuse to send his best agents to every part of the 
globe. Monopoly over the powers of the Aos is much more than simple 
propaganda, but rather one of the most important long-term objectives 
of the organization.
      The main headquarters of the Arcana Familia is in London, in - and 
especially under - the ancient Cathedral of St Paul.
In addition to this main site, smaller Familia branches are scattered 
throughout the Earth Union.
      Being an army corps, the Arcana Familia enjoys many privileges, 
especially compared to the normal police. However, a wise Lost Child 
knows that it is never a good idea to push people too much or be too 
much in the spotlight: the risk is attracting unwanted attention to 
themselves and to the entire organization. Furthermore, the Familia al­
ready has enough enemies without having to add angry police inspectors 
to the list!
      That said, here is a list of what a Familia agent on an “official” mission 
can do:
     -a Lost Child can move freely around any crime scene and consult the 
police records freely.
      -a Lost Child can at any time remove the police from a case and 
investigating on their own, but is required to deliver their conclusions 
to the authorities as soon as possible, so that they can close the case and 
proceed with any arrest. 
      -a Lost Child can withhold and interrogate a witness or suspect, but is 
expected not to inflict any kind of violence - physical or otherwise - on 
the person in their custody (with the powers of the Aos at its disposal, 
the Familia often ignore this little clause). The person should also be 
held for no more than an hour, and any discovery or statement should 
quickly be reported to the competent authorities.
      -a Lost Child can search any building or vehicle, just like a policeman, 
but first they must obtain a warrant from the local police. Generally, to 
Familia agents are asked much less questions than to a normal detective, 
but they are expected to provide at least a logical reason.
     -a Lost Child can temporarily requisition any object linked to an 
investigation, but must deliver it as soon as possible to the authorities 
in charge. Obviously this object will be cataloged, and the Familia will be 
held responsible for any damage or disappearance of the 
aforementioned.
      -a Lost Child has no right to arrest a suspect, but can hand them over - 
along with obvious evidence of guilt - to the local police .
      -a Lost Child can break into any building, if there is a situation of 
obvious emergency, where there are actual dangers to human life.
      The Exchange, in addition to granting immortality - for what it’s 
worth - to a human being, also gives them some undeniable advantages. 
The Lost Children are generally stronger, faster and more sturdy than 
the average person, in addition to being immune to almost all known 
diseases and drugs. However, Lost Children also have more mystical 
advantages, that no technology can ever replicate: the Aos in fact grants 
their human partner a fraction of their powers, which they can use 
whenever they want to accomplish feats that go beyond the limits of the 
human possibilities.

AOS
     The “race” of the Aos is actually a group composed of members of 
many different species from all over the universe, all united by a single 
factor - an event that occurred during their more or less long mortal life: 
they once made an Exchange with an Aos that arrived on their world.
The transformation - or Ascension, as they call it - of an Aos is the final 
process, at least as far as the Aos themselves know, that every creature 
who has stipulated an Exchange must necessarily face. It can be 
postponed, but never canceled. If the Lost Children survive long enough, 
it will also be their destiny. A destiny as inevitable and already written as 
that to which Many Names had destined them - an irony the Aos never 
fail to notice, even if they cannot share it with their human “friends”. 
      Having already been through it, most of the Aos observe human 
affairs with a mixture of amusement and pity, as they see dynamics 
already seen over and over again in thousands of other worlds 
before ours taking place again before their eyes, like an old play whose 
scripts they know by heart. This, however, does not mean that they are 
apathetic creatures - at least in most cases. Many of them still have the 
desire to become truly free, and every new world discovered through 
Wonderland is a new opportunity for them to dismantle once and for 
all the Aos system and defeat both The Nameless One and Many Names 
- the former for exploiting their weaknesses and their dreams to make 
them slaves, and the latter for having pushed them into the arms of The 
Nameless One with their terrifying fate. The Aos generally do not care 
that they have already failed in innumerable worlds; there are countless 
others, and two things these entities certainly do not lack are guinea pigs 
and time.
      The Aos cannot manifest themselves with their real appearance 
outside of Wonderland - at least for the moment. The only way they can 
interact with human world is through the Mask.
      This term refers to a partial manifestation of the Aos, limited by the 
world in which the creature manifests themselves. 
      The Mask is exactly what the name implies: a disguise that hides the 
true face of the Aos. It can be similar to what it hides, or instead appear 
diametrically opposed. As with the true appearance of an Aos, the 
players are free to be creative with their Mask. They have a single limit: 
a Mask always suits the world in which it is born. Therefore, the Mask of 
an Aos cannot contain materials not present on Earth, nor can it violate 
the laws of logic and physics. It must be something a normal human 
being can give a rational explanation for. For example, a Mask could 
be a particularly large cat with reddish eyes, but not a cat as big as an 
elephant and with three heads. 
GAME RULES
Arcana Familia has a dual system, meaning that the characters will 
always be characterized by a couple formed by a Lost Child and the Aos 
with whom they made the pact called Exchange. A Lost Child cannot 
exist without an Aos, and an Aos has no relevance as a PC in an Arcana 
Familia Adventure without a Lost Child. Basically, two players will be 
paired, one playing a Lost Child and the other the Aos to whom he is 
bonded.
LOST CHILD
ATTRIBUTES: The intrinsic abilities of the character, such as physical 
strength, charisma, willpower or intelligence. The Attributes are divided 
into Physical, Social and Mental. 
SKILLS: The experiences accumulated over the course of life, useful for 
carrying out tasks and activities in the most efficient way possible, and 
the knowledge acquired in each area. They are Assemble, Knowledge, 
Command, Fight, Dexterity, Aesthetics, Crafts, Interact, Search. 
PECULIARITIES: The Peculiarities represent other elements that 
contribute to characterize the character further and that come into play 
in specific circumstances. Each Peculiarity has its own cost. Various 
examples are Wealth, Contacts, Languages, Specialization, Diehard, 
Ambidextrous, Martial Arts.
BLOOD: Equal to 10. This represents the amount of blood that can be 
used by Lost Children and by their Aos to activate the powers conferred 
by the Contracts made.
HEALTH: Equal to 6. How many injuries the character can suffer before 
becoming helpless. 
Ending at Health 0:  When the character drops to 0 Health, they be­
come Helpless. A Helpless character loses consciousness and is unable 
to perform actions.
  If a Helpless character suffers further wounds or their Health drops 
below zero and thus reaches a negative value, they must make an Avera­
ge Difficulty Physical Attribute Challenge (called Game with Death) 
every turn during a Conflict or every two seconds (if not in Conflict), 
as long as they remain at Health 0 and their condition is not stabilized. 
Each failure increases the difficulty of the next Challenge by one rank. 
When a Very Hard Challenge is failed, the character dies. Each Health 
point that would be lost while the character is helpless increases the 
difficulty of the Challenge by one rank.   The Challenges are interrupted 
once the character’s condition is stabilized thanks to medical aid throu­
gh a Challenge of Mental/Crafts skills (Medicine Specialization contri­
butes if one has it) with difficulty equal to that of the Game with Death 
that should be made that minute. The character is no longer considered 
Helpless once Health returns above 0.
AOS
DYNASTY: The Aos Dynasty to which the Aos is associated. They are 
Sovereign, Shaper, Devourer, Consecrated and Solitary
CONCEPTS: The area of influence of the Aos Contracts. 
STATUS: The importance of an Aos in the Wonderland hierarchy. 
Determines the Attributes of  their Mask ( equal to the rating), their 
Blood Reserve (5 x rating, which also counts as the Mask’s Health) and 
the power of their Contracts.

CONTRACTS: The powers an Aos grants to their Lost Children.. All Aos 
have for free Dimensional Telepathy. Minor Contracts include Energy 
Shock, Restoration, Reinforcement, Weakening, Clairvoyance and 
Scenic Effect. The Major ones include Animate, Creation, Extinction, 
Immortal, Teleportation and Summon. An Aos can use Contracts only 
through their Lost Child, but they can cause their activation even if the 
Lost Child doesn’t agree  – and can also not allow them to be activated no 
matter the Lost Child’s will. Each use requires the expense of the blood 
of the Lost Child or the Aos. 
Following are the powers granted by the Aos presented in this Quick­
starter
- Dimensional Telepathy
      The Aos can communicate telepathically with their Lost Child (and 
vice versa) at any distance. If they focus enough, they can also experience 
all the sensations their Lost Child is feeling. 
      In cases of extreme necessity, the Aos can use Dimensional Telepathy 
to impose a Command on their Lost Child,  who is obliged to obey as 
quickly as possible and as accurately as possible, even if this threatens 
their situation or even their physical integrity: this power requires that 
the Aos spend all its Blood Reserve, thus destroying its Mask.
      Dimensional Telepathy permits also to recognize the Mask of another 
Aos they’re sensing with the senses of their own Mask or through their 
Lost Child and communicate telepathically with them.
-Reinforcement
      The Lost Child can enhance the abilities and strength of an object, 
creature or phenomenon within 5m and associated to the Aos Concept, 
increasing by (Status) a characteristic of their choosing (Attribute or 
Skill) for an hour. When used on something that has no characteristic, 
the GM determines the consequences of the upgrade based on its in­
tensity (for example, with a boost of 1 the flame of a match can become 
comparable to a bonfire, of 2 to a blowtorch, of 3 to a fire, of 4 to a blast 
furnace). Not cumulative. It costs 4 Blood points.
-Clairvoyance / Clairaudience
      The Aos can see with the senses of an entity linked to their concept 
within 100m from the Lost Child, for a maximum time frame of 5 
minutes. If the entity does not have sensory organs, it is considered as 
if the object could see through its surface, but any challenge to carefully 
observe something ia made at a difficult two levels higher  than normal. 
Challenges made to observe any details or to distinguish what is 
happening in confusing environments are considered as made using a 
score equal to the lower between the Status and the Attribute itself. It 
costs 4 Blood points.
-Immortal
The Lost Child plays the Final Game with Death (Very Hard) at Hard 
Difficulty. If they fail, they die.   Furthermore, even when they’re hel­
pless, they remain conscious and can perform actions. However, as long 
as they’re in this state, they skip the Elimination Phase of any Challenge 
(except for the Game with Death).
  It has no Blood cost and is always active.
THE BLACKJACK SYSTEM
   Whenever a character needs to determine the success of their action, 
they must win a challenge. Generally it requires the use of a poker deck 
(one for each player and one for the Game Master). Basically, this deck 
(called Fate Deck) contains all the cards numbered from 1 to 10 of each 
suit, meaning all the face cards and jokers are removed. Depending 
on the character’s traits, the Fate Deck can be modified. A challenge is 
generally divided into the following phases: 
1) Declaration: The player declares the action they want to take. If this is 
not already stated by the rules, the GM indicates which Attributes and 
Skills one must refer to (the player can give suggestions about it) and the 
difficulty of the challenge. The player can Step Up the Action, 
considering the Attribute lower by a chosen value to obtain a correspon­
ding bonus in case of success (such as Damage in an Attack)
2) Shuffle: The deck is shuffled, so that the cards are always drawn 
randomly. 
3) First Draw: The first two cards of the deck are drawn and placed on 
the table. 
4) Next Draw: Additional cards are drawn one at a time, up to a 
maximum number of cards determined by the appropriate modifiers 
(generally the Attributes). The draw can be interrupted before reaching 
the maximum number of cards allowed when and if deemed appro­
priate. Once this is done, players move on to the next phase without the 
possibility of drawing additional cards (unless explicitly specified).
5) Elimination: A number of revealed cards determined by the appro­
priate modifiers (generally Skills and Peculiarities) are chosen and 
removed from the table, no longer influencing the final result. 
6) Calculation: The value of the cards is summed up, to see if the cha­
racter reaches the minimum required by the test (Very easy 14, Easy 16, 
Medium 18, Difficult 19, Very Difficult 20) and if doesn’t exceed 21. 
If the result is within this range, it is considered that the challenge has 
been successful, while if the result is lower than the minimum rating 
required by the challenge, it is considered as failed. If the results exceeds 
21, the test is considered failed due to an excess of energy used to achieve 
the goal – normally with worse consequences than a simple failure (this 
type of failure is called Critical Failure). A result of 21 reached before the 
Elimination phase is considered an extraordinary success and is treated 
as if you had Step Up the Action by one greater degree. The aces can be 
worth 1 or 11, as desired. The face cards are worth 10.
7) Repositioning: The cards are collected and reshuffled in the deck.

8) Conclusion: The GM describes the consequences of the action based

      When a challenge involves a reaction from the target of the action (for 
example, if a PC tries to intimidate a NPC in order to make them obey 
their order), an opposing challenge occurs. In this case, all those involved 
(both PCs and NPCs) try a challenge, in which modifiers (Attributes and

wins the challenge. In the event of a tie, the top card of each participant’s 
deck is revealed and the player with the highest score wins. Note: for 
a matter of speed, in case the challenge is not carried out in a topical 
moment such as a fight, it is recommended for GMs to also consider 
those that would be opposing challenges as normal challenges, assigning 
a difficulty proportionate to the Attributes that the NPC involved would 
possess.
Example: Clint has to reach an upper floor three meters high, so he

COMBAT
     Combat is divided into turns. The order in which the characters 
involved act is determined in descending order based on their Mental 
Attribute (plus various modifiers), with possible tiebreaker. In a turn, a 
character can perform one of the following actions: Move, Action, 
Charged Attack, Targeted Attack, Guard, Rush, Feint and Attack. An 
attack consists of a opposing challenge based on Physical/Combat. If it 
is an attack with a ranged weapon, the attacker (from now on, for con­
venience, the attacking character will be called the attacker, and the one 
being attacked the defender) take the challenge on Physical/
Dexterity. If the attack succeeds, the defender loses Health equal to the 
Damage rating indicated for the weapon once subtracted the Protection 
rating conferred by any equipment or ability the defender may possess 
at the moment. Situations such as attacking from behind or using two 
weapons at the same time can apply modifiers to the challenges.
--Movement 
      The characters generally move up to (Physical +1) meters. It is possible 
to make an extra effort to move further with a Physical/Dexterity 
Medium Challenge (the difference between the result and 16 is the 
additional meters one can move), but the next action will have a negative 
modifier of 1 to the phase of Elimination - a malus that will be applied 
every time that an additional effort will be made during this Combat 
and also later, as long as the participant does not catch their breath 
(which they can do by using an action).
--Action
      The participant makes an attack, activates a Contract or performs 
another type of action, for which the GM decides whether a challenge 
of some kind is required (for example, extracting a weapon or jumping 
over an obstacle). Catching one’s breath is considered an action. Certain 
actions may require more than one phase to be completed.
- Loaded Attack
      The participant makes a non-ranged attack: for this attack the weapon 
is considered to have a +1 to damage. If attacked before a new Phase can 
be carried out, in the challenge that follows, the character skips the Next 
Draw and Elimination phases.
- Targeted Attack
The participant takes aim at a target with a ranged weapon. During his 
next Turn phase, if not distracted or interrupted (for example, suffering 
an attack), they can make a ranged attack with a +1 damage bonus.
--Guard
The participant takes a guard position. If attacked, they can add +1 to 
their positive Next Draw and Elimination modifiers. 
--Rush
The participant moves (Physical +1) meters in a straight line and makes 
an attack, but sacrifices the Elimination phase.
- Feint and Attack
The participant makes a Mental/Fight challenge against an adjacent 
target. If the attacker wins, they immediately execute an attack in which 
the defender skips the elimination phase. If the attacker loses, they 
execute an attack in which they skip their Elimination phase.

ADVENTURE EXAMPLE
HUNT IN THE ALLEYS
      In Nuova Este, the megalopolis in what was once Veneto and Emilia 
Romagna (in Italy) and which covers the territory from Ferrara to 
the borders of Padua, for several months now there have been serial 
murders in which the victims have been partially devoured. Police have 
obtained no result in their investigations. However, on the basis of little 
information received  the Arcana Familia suspects the influence of an 
Aos in the whole matter. For this reason, when the next murder is 
reported, a team is sent to the scene to investigate.
PRELIMINARY REPORT
      Largely incomplete, much of the information is vague or refers to 
documentation that was not included in the file delivered to the agency. 
An Average Mental / Search Challenge makes it possible to conclude that 
this is not due to an attempt to sabotage or cover up the investigation, 
but rather to sloppiness and lack of commitment. Including the current 
one, the victims are a total of eight, and they are all residents of the area 
where the killings took place - a fairly degraded popular residential area 
basically abandoned by the city administration. Curiously, it was repor­
ted as a low priority case, despite the type of crime.
THE SCENE OF THE EIGHTH CRIME
      The scene is located in a labyrinth of alleys intertwining among 
several low-level apartment buildings of workers and employees, inhabi­
ted mostly by homeless people. TThe dead is a woman is in her twenties, 
dressed in revealing clothes: her choice of outfit, however, does not seem 
dictated by aesthetics, but from a desire to use sensuality to accentuate 
an aggressive and dangerous around her, as if her look was meant to 
subdue any interlocutor by any means. Her skin is completely covered 
in tattoos, and she has low-level cybernetic implants that have been 
modified to be flashy and look prestigious. She carried a gun and a knife, 
found in the vicinity of her body. Her body was torn apart, with her rib 
cage completely open and her internal organs absent or mangled. 
      The agents present seem quite indifferent to the scene, sometimes 
going so far as to make irreverent comments about the deceased. 
They do not seem to show interest or commitment in carrying out the 
investigation, merely taking a few pictures and making a few notes. If 
asked, they will be courteous towards the Lost Children, but will try in 
every way to convince them that it is not necessary to spend time and 
resources to find the culprit of these murders. If pressed about it, with a 
Hard Social / Interact Challenge, they will reveal that all the dead belong 
to two gangs of thugs and drug dealers, the Red Cobras and the Third 
District: from their point of view in a way whoever is responsible for this 
slaughter is doing a favor to the neighborhood.
      By examining the area, with an Easy Mental / Aesthetics Challenge, 
one can understand that the victim, before being killed, was running 
away from something; with a Hard Challenge instead it is evident that 
there are various elements out of place - overturned bins, broken bottles 
and so on - but they are all traces left by the victim, leading to assume 
that her pursuer was exceptionally agile and graceful. Questioning 
some of the homeless in the alleyways – a Medium Challenge of Social 
/ Interact - will lead the characters to the same conclusions. The few 
homeless people living in these alleys witnessed the crime, but they 
seem quite confused about what they actually saw: players will be forced 
to interpret their words to extrapolate the information. They saw the 
woman running away, and they are sure that something was chasing her, 
even though they saw nothing. They heard heavy footsteps crashing into 
puddles, as well as a current of air.
      Finally, the Aos will perceive the presence in the vicinity of one of 
their own, even if they are unable to determine in which direction and 
their distance.
DIFFICULTIES IN FORMAL INVESTIGATIONS 
      Even if a request is made about it perfectly following the entire pro­
cedure, and the agents use all their charisma and their clever words to 
convince their interlocutors to assist them, requests for documentation 
of all kinds - reports on individual investigations, coroner’s analyzes, the 
statistics on the neighborhood and more - will tend to proceed 
extremely slowly or to get lost in the maze of bureaucracy, if not to 
actually fall into oblivion. Individual officiers cannot explain why even 
under supernatural coercion or manipulation, as they are unaware of 
the whole picture: all they will give will be incomplete or erroneous 
reasons. In fact, behind the whole situation there is more than the lack of 
interest in the victims; is in fact underway a confidential investigation, 
complete with undercover agents, that should lead to very 
important arrests against the two gangs, inflicting considerable damage 
on criminal world in the neighborhood. Department leaders fear that 
the investigation into the murders could alert the gangsters, and 
therefore try not to jeopardize the case. Unfortunately for them, the 
Arcana Familia is an agency independent from them that has no interest 
in the investigation of the common police: even if they were asked to 
step aside, they could just ignore it. A collaboration is to be excluded too, 
as the officers have no intention of sharing the merit of their operation 
with outsiders – they even fear to see the merit would be stolen from 
under their nose. For this reason, not only are the investigations of the 
Lost Children hindered, but there will be constant pressure (even if ne­
ver in a hostile way) for the case to be shelved. Moreover, if the perpetra­
tors are not the members of the gangs themselves, it must be a vigilante, 
and so far their work not only does not invalidate the investigation but 
rather creates a fuss useful to hide the operation in progress (none of the 
infiltrators was a victim of the ‘murderer). For this reason, the agents 
will be forced to collect all the information in person or, if it is contained 
in the police archives, to have to steal it without being discovered 
(according to the rules of the protocol, agents can officially 
request - even putting pressure - but they must still use the local police as 
intermediaries).

THE GANGS
       Unable to rely on the police, agents will find themselves forced to 
gather information from the gangs to which the victims directly belon­
ged. These, of course, will be openly hostile towards the Arcana Familia, 
wanting to resolve the matter completely on their own and having no

Low-level members can try to intimidate the agents with threats and

lawyers. Therefore the characters will have to be exceptionally

      Red Cobras are generally of Asian ethnicity (although in this 
historical period it is mostly a distant ancestry) and are organized as a 
pyramidal Triad; their aesthetic refers to the Wuxia style, even if in a 
more modern and extreme cheap take. 
      The Third District is an alliance of smaller bands, each of which is 
characterized by its own territories and its own rituals; inside you can 
find a bit of everything, from tattooed warriors inspired by African 
culture to neo-pagans who refer to the Vikings. They are commanded by 
a council made up of representatives of each individual band, 
characterized by a totemic animal.

a war that has not yet started because they fear they will be open against 
a possible third participant, but the breaking point is getting nearer with 
every passing day. The deaths on the other side are justified as purging of 
weak or untrusted components, in order to take advantage to strengthen 
internal cohesion and tighten ranks against a common enemy.
      Overall, the victims don’t have much in common with each other, 
other than being part of the two gangs. The only information useful 
for the investigation concerns the movements of the victims before 
the attack (they were all in local areas relaxing or having fun) and that 
the female victims were bisexual, pansexual or lesbian, suggesting that 
there is a good possibility that the culprit is a “black widow”. The Game

derail. Investigating the five different bars (by questioning regulars and 
bartenders, studying surveillance videos, or other ways the players will 
come up with), they will discover that all the victims have been approa­
ched by particularly uninhibited and seductive women. Each of them is 
different tough: no one seems to recognize them and facial recognition 
programs cannot identify them. A Hard Challenge on Mental/ Com­
mand or Mental / Interact while watching the videos allows the

idiosyncrasies and the same tics. Furthermore, all people questioned

remembered having ever seen her before. It is in fact the same woman, 
Lamia Ezzini, whose appearance is disguised each time in a supernatural 
way. If the agents come to the conclusion that the person responsible is 
the same person, they can come to identify her in different ways. 
Certainly setting a trap for her would have no effect; her Aos has sensed 
the presence of his fellow Aos, and ordered her to hide and stop their 
activities for the moment. One way would be, using the information

being chased by a maniac who wanted to torture her to death. He used 
his Lost Child to lure his victims, individuals who believed themselves 
powerful by virtue of their violence and influence, to secluded places set 
up to serve as hunting grounds, then hunt them down like animals and 
cause in them terror – terror he fed on along with their internal organs.     
      Arriving at the scene, they will find the Aos, a saber-toothed tiger the 
size of a small car, and his Lost Child discussing the situation; she will 
show a total dependence and submission to the Aos, as if he had 
completely broken her will. When the agents reveal themselves, 
Kanavath will make himself invisible ((emptying of all energies Lamia,

the invaders. The characters can decide to destroy his Mask or try to 
subdue him diplomatically, perhaps taking advantage of Lamia’s 
inability to defend herself (even  if the Aos has no respect or affection 
for his Lost Child, her death would lead to his exile in Wonderland).
      At this point the agents can then close the case, delivering Lamia (dead 
or alive), Kavanath or both to the Arcana Familia and drawing up the 
report on the whole case.
