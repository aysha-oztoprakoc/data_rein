BATTLE VEST
THE ARMOR OF THE 
NOISE THRASHER
The go to armor of the Noise Thrasher that grants them a +3 to their Armor Class.
Infused with a strange radioactive magical force, the Battle Vest grants the Noise Thrasher some minor bonuses based 
on the band patch they add. Being considered “magical” the Brutacorn can also wear a Battle vest in lieu of their Leather 
Jacket. When a Noise Thrasher dies if anyone puts it on to gain the magical bonuses must roll on the Minor Mutation 
Table.
BATTLE VEST PATCHES
Each Noise Thrasher begins with one patch on their vest. At every level advancement the Noise Thrasher can gain an 
additional patch in exchange for any advancement in Hit Points.
