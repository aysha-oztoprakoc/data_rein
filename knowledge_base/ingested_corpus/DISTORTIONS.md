Distortions 
Also known as: shadows, mirrors, id’s, reflections, hollows, 
other selves 
 
A Distortion is the true self, made manifest via a tear in the 
Psychic Sea. Beyond mere Drifters, they are somewhere between an 
extension of an exorcist’s own grace and a proper sin. They are 
always tied to a specific exorcist who already has a strong 
connection to the psychic sea. 
 
FACT: No exorcist under CAT III has ever been recorded 
manifesting a Distortion. Research ongoing if high lethality 
rates obscure data collection. 
 
Distortions in Hunts 
 
When the exorcists arrive at 
the investigation area of any 
hunt, the admin may choose 
one exorcist to manifest a 
Distortion of, called the 
distorted host. A psychic 
entity unrelated to the main 
sin will set up an additional 
Distorted Palace in the 
investigation area. 
 
The characters may not know a 
Distortion is present, but 
the players should always be 
aware that a Distortion is 
present at deployment. The 
identity of the distorted 
host may or may not be 
revealed as well. 
 
Even if the players are 
unaware of which exorcist 
will manifest a Distortion, 
the admin should know the 
following information about 
the distorted host prior to 
the hunt beginning: 
 
●​ What sin type would you 
turn into if you 
succumbed to sin this 
hunt? 
●​ What’s one thing that 
would leave you 
vulnerable in a fight if 
exploited? 
 
It is possible for there to 
be more than one Distortion 
present in a single hunt, 
each with their own Distorted 
Palace. This is not 
recommended. 
 
FACT: Most blasphemies fall within a single standard deviation 
of the mean manifestation rate. The most extreme outliers are 
Whisper users, manifesting Distortions 25% less frequently than 
the average, and Jaunt users, manifesting Distortions at nearly 
40% above the expected rate.

Statblock 
 
Distortion (sin). Execution Talisman: (distorted host’s CAT + 
distorted host’s current sin). 
 
If the distorted host’s sin reduces, the Distortion’s execution 
talisman reduces with it. This can never mark its final slash 
on the execution talisman. It has the following features: 
 
●​ Is the same CAT as the distorted host. 
●​ Possesses a single Domain from the distorted host’s future 
sin, the type they declared they would become upon 
succumbing to sin. 
●​ Possesses a number of Distorted Domains equal to ½ CAT. 
●​ Takes -1 slash from all sources other than the distorted 
host. 
●​ Always knows the precise location, emotional state, and 
current objective of the distorted host. 
 
Reactions: 
 
Inflict stress. Attacks with: distorted host’s weapons, psyche 
blast (½ CAT range), CAIN-standard martial arts, insults from 
the heart (1): 4 stress, (2/3): 3 stress, (4+): 2 stress 
 
Or: create a complication or threat: perfectly duplicate the 
distorted host, read the distorted host like a book, isolate 
the distorted host, tell someone what the distorted host really 
thinks, destroy what the distorted host cares for, draw the 
attention of the primary sin 
 
Or: Distort the Self (1): The Distortion fades, psychically 
overshadowing the distorted host’s psyche within a short range. 
The Distortion can only take this reaction when the distorted 
host would die, submit during sin overflow, or when their Real 
Me hook is filled. 
 
If the Distort the Self reaction is in response to an exorcist 
dying or submitting during sin overflow, it saves their life as 
they instead go to 1 stress or 1 sin below their maximum.

Sparing a Distortion 
 
Once a Distortion’s execution talisman is reduced to 0, it can 
be executed or spared. After executing or sparing a Distortion, 
all surviving exorcists gain 3 scrip at the end of the mission 
as hazard pay in addition to their other rewards.  
 
Sparing a Distortion requires answering all of its Trauma 
Questions publicly, honestly, and to the Distortion directly. 
Once its execution talisman is 0, the distorted host can spend 
their action forcing the Distortion to ask one of its Trauma 
Questions, potentially dealing nonlethal stress to the distorted 
host if they don’t answer. 
 
An exorcist who spares their Distortion finds a calm in the 
psychic sea, able to assert their psyche over humanity’s stain. 
They gain the Last Gasp ability and will never go hollow. 
 
Last Gasp. After succumbing to sin and becoming an Imago, the 
exorcist can momentarily reassert their control over their new 
form. For the remainder of that scene, they are in full control 
of their actions as an Imago. After the scene ends, they make a 
1d6 fortune roll. On a 6, they manage to revert back to a human 
form as if they had managed to resist the previous roll. They 
roll for a sin mark like normal. 
 
A single exorcist may only use Last Gasp once, and a Distortion 
spared can never manifest again. 
 
FACT: Distortions not executed or spared demanifest 
automatically upon the distorted host’s exfiltration. 
 
66% of such cases result in a manifestation on their following 
deployment.

Personality 
 
Distortions are based on the innermost desires and subconscious 
of the distorted host, and will act accordingly. They might: 
 
●​ Negotiate with or appeal to the emotions of the other 
exorcists 
●​ Ambush the exorcists while they’re fighting the sin 
●​ Leverage traps or mundane obstacles to exhaust the 
exorcists’ resources 
●​ Attack them directly and as often as possible 
 
However, a Distortion always 
has the following objective 
that overwrites even the 
strongest convictions of the 
distorted host: 
 
Replace the distorted host. 
 
If the distorted host dies or 
submits during sin overflow 
within a short range of the 
Distortion, it can make the 
Distort the Self reaction for 
free. Alternatively, it can 
take the same reaction when 
the distorted host rolls a 1 
on the risk die if they have 
a filled out Real Me hook. 
 
Such “Distorted” exorcists 
act much the same as they did 
prior to encountering their 
Distortion, though with a 
stronger dogma in their 
convictions and a loosening 
of inhibitions. Their general 
personalities are the same, 
just “sharpened” or 
“hollowed.” 
 
The Distorted exorcist 
immediately changes their 
Agenda to the Distortion 
agenda and gains one of its 
abilities. They may only swap 
off of it by spending two 
advances, as CAIN assists 
them to deradicalize (but 
never completely). 
 
No such exorcist has ever 
manifested another 
Distortion, nor gone hollow. 
Even Jaunt users. 
 
FACT: “Distorted” exorcists are considered pseudo Type-II sins 
and marked for indefinite detainment outside of special 
circumstances. 
 
See log CD-9B1 for details and permission slip application.

The Distorted Palace is a 
twisted reflection of the 
distorted host’s ideal rest

When entering it for the

●​ What was the Palace

●​ How is the Palace being

Real Me. Every time the

Agendas 
 
The Distortion gains 2 Trauma Questions from the list below. 
 
●​ What did you do to deserve this life? 
●​ What would have you betray your fellow exorcists? 
●​ Why not just give in and become a sin? 
●​ Why aren’t you a good person? 
●​ What’s the one thing about yourself you’d never tell a 
soul? 
 
Then, the Distortion gains 1 more Trauma Question associated 
with the distorted host’s current agenda (see the next page). 
 
Using Trauma Questions 
 
Whenever the distorted host takes an action in a scene where the 
Distortion is present, it can force them to answer one of the 
Trauma Questions. They must publicly answer it truthfully or 
else take 1d3 nonlethal stress and reduce their successes by the 
same amount. 
 
The Distortion is free to add additional context or questions, 
but the core question is the only one that requires answering. 
 
A Distortion can only use each Trauma Question once per hunt. 
However, the distorted host can answer Trauma Questions after 
they have already been asked in order to spare the Distortion. 
 
A Distortion with 0 slashes left on its execution talisman is 
always willing to repeat Trauma Questions if prompted, dealing 
no additional stress even if not answered. 
 
SEER has found that disability or impairment have no impact on 
the requirements of a “public” answer. Writing, sign language, 
or utilizing blasphemies all suffice in place of speech. 
 
The only requirement appears to be a cognitive shift within the 
distorted host where they believe they revealed the answer.

Distorted Domains 
 
The Distortion possesses a number of Distorted Domains equal to 
½ CAT. All Distorted Domains must be chosen from a blasphemy the 
distorted host has access to or the generic Distorted Domains. 
 
If the distorted host only has blasphemies other than those 
listed here, the admin is free to stick to the generic Distorted 
Domains or to simply make their own. 
 
Reverences 
 
Distorted Domains can also function as representations of 
blasphemy powers Binders might use. Specifically, 
bastardizations of the Twelve used to counter and respond-to 
CAIN-sanctioned exorcists. 
 
However, these so-called “Reverences” come with some changes: 
 
●​ Any reference to CAT always uses the Binder’s CAT, even if 
the power mentions relying on the distorted host’s. 
●​ At the beginning of each scene, the Binder chooses one 
exorcist as their prey. Any reference to targeting the 
distorted host instead targets the Binder’s prey. 
●​ If a Reverence relies on specific abilities the target or 
distorted host has, the Binder cannot target an exorcist 
that does not have those abilities. 
●​ Any reference to a Distorted Palace uses the Binder’s 
psychic palace, as per the Palace blasphemy. Often referred 
to as a psychic “Cell” or “Safehouse” by binders. 
●​ Any reference to the Real Me hook assumes it is full.  
 
They don’t function like powers from CAIN-standardized 
blasphemies, they function like sin domains. 
 
And many of their practitioners prefer it that way. 
 
FACT: Fighting a Binder who cannot target you with their 
Reverences is preferable to fighting one who can.

GENERIC 
Spare Not the Still-Weeping Young 
 
The Distortion grows stronger as it causes harm to those around 
it. Whenever the Distortion kills a human or inflicts an injury 
on an exorcist, it recovers one slash on its execution 
talisman. It cannot gain more than CAT slashes per scene. 
 
When inflicting an injury on the distorted host, they increment 
their Real Me hook by 1 if it isn’t already full. If it is, 
they gain 1 sin. 
Beyond Reality, Regret, and Reason 
 
The Distortion is only faintly held onto the distorted host, 
melding the line between the psychic sea and the real world. 
Drifters and traces are naturally drawn to the Distortion and 
will likely be found in its Distorted Palace. They may be 
hostile to the Distortion, but always prioritize attacking the 
exorcists over it. 
 
As a complication, the Distortion can fade into the psychic sea 
and turn nearly invisible. It is impossible to harm with 
mundane weapons and is completely ignored by sins for the 
duration. It is forced to return to reality upon taking damage 
and may choose to return early. 
The Wayward Offspring of Woe and Weal 
 
The Distortion radiates the innermost feelings of the distorted 
host directly into others’ psyches. Whenever an exorcist spends 
a psyche burst in CAT range, they may choose to gain both 1 sin 
and a ‘Weal’ hook. When the hook fills out, they regain all of 
their psyche bursts. 
 
Whenever an exorcist deals a slash to the Distortion’s 
execution talisman, they take 1 sin. If it’s the distorted 
host, they instead take 1d3 stress. 
An Inability To Pity the Wretched 
 
When an exorcist enters the Distorted Palace for the first time 
or rolls a 1 on the risk die while within it, they increment a 
‘Pity’ hook. When it fills, they take one affliction of the 
Distortion’s choice from the distorted host’s future sin.

TENSION 
When Confronted With Truth, You Shield Your Eyes 
 
The Distortion is clad in psychic armor, shielding it from 
harm. It no longer takes -1 slash from all actions other than 
the distorted host. Instead, it takes -1 slash from the 
distorted host and reduces all slashes from other sources by 
1d3 (to a minimum of 0). 
 
As a complication, the Distortion can reform its armor into a 
jagged amalgamation of blades. Any exorcist that attacks it 
takes 1 stress for every 1 they roll, not including the risk 
die. The armor fades after dealing an amount of stress equal to 
or exceeding CAT. 
These Crumbling Towers of Salt and Sand 
 
Drawing upon a wellspring of psychic force, the Distortion can 
manifest physical barriers to ward off foes. As a complication, 
the Distortion can create a mental block up to CAT size within 
a short range. 
 
Once per hunt, as a complication, the Distortion can create a 
series of psychic walls around the distorted host. This 
includes a ceiling and floor. While within these mental blocks, 
the distorted host cannot spend or gain psyche bursts for any 
reason but can still use blasphemies by gaining sin. 
 
Mental blocks are impenetrable to mundane harm, but have a 
talisman equal to CAT for psychic damage. The Distortion can 
move freely through its barriers. They can be anywhere between 
opaque and transparent, of any color of the Distortion’s 
choice, and are always a sheer surface. 
The Heart On My Sleeve, A Scabbard 
 
Any time the Distortion takes an action that would be 
obstructed by a Tension power, the admin rolls a 1d6 fortune 
die. On a 2-5, the Distortion forces its way through the power 
but reduces any stress dealt by 1. On a 1, the Distortion 
ignores the power completely and dispels it. 
 
Whenever the Distortion takes 2 or more slashes from an action, 
the risk die is rolled twice and the lower result is taken.

ARDENCE 
Hell is Frozen, Heaven is Burning 
 
The Distortion alters the temperature around it to be 
uncomfortable at best and harmful at worst. A CAT area around 
the Distortion becomes scalding hot, freezing cold, or whatever 
climate is least hospitable to the distorted host. This may 
make rolls risky or hard when in the area. 
 
As a reaction, the climate flares with intensity. All exorcists 
in the area take 1d3 stress, or 1 nonlethal stress if they are 
in appropriate attire. Humans in the area may suffer from heat 
stroke, hypothermia, or even instant death. 
 
If an Ardence power is used in this area, the user must roll a 
1d6 fortune die. On a 1, the power indiscriminately targets 
everything in a range of the user’s CAT+2, centered on them. 
The Thunderous Fury of An Unquiet Mind 
 
Altering the air pressure, the Distortion releases the tension 
wound within a vile secret to disastrous effect. The Distortion 
gains the Repulsive Thought reaction: 
 
Repulsive Thought (1-2). Any exorcist in a short range is blown 
backwards and takes 1d3+1 stress. Additionally, they have some 
complication in returning to where they were (blown off a 
building, sprained an ankle, knocked under debris). The 
distorted host’s player must reveal what the secret was. 
Burn Upon The Pyre Of Your Hubris 
 
The Distortion incorporates flickers of psychic energy into 
itself to vastly increase its range. The Distortion’s attacks 
increase to the distorted host’s CAT. 
 
Additionally, as a threat, it can choose an exorcist. If the 
exorcist is not taken out of line of sight before the threat 
resolves, they take 2d3 stress and are engulfed in psychic 
energy. Every turn they or another exorcist doesn’t take an 
action to put them out, they take an additional 2 stress. 
 
When the Distortion’s execution talisman is filled, every 
exorcist in a short range immediately takes 2d3 stress in an 
explosion of psychic energy. Humans die painfully and messily.

FLUX 
Living In The Past Won’t Change It 
 
The Distortion clings to the past, refusing to allow success to 
alter it. Once per round, the Distortion can force an exorcist 
to reroll one die from a successful action they just took, 
including the risk die. The second roll must be used. 
 
Once per hunt, when the Distortion’s Talisman is filled up, it 
reverses time to the moment before it was slain. The exorcist 
must reroll all of their dice, including the risk die, except 
now it is hard. If it was already hard, it becomes impossible. 
Time Is A River, I Am Its Dam 
 
The Distortion can pause itself or others in time, seemingly 
“floating” in midair or otherwise defying conventional 
causality. In CAT range, using Flux powers always makes the 
user gain 1 temporal instability if they otherwise gained none. 
 
Once per round, after an exorcist declares their action, the 
Distortion can attempt to freeze them in time. The target rolls 
PSYCHE. If they fail, they must go last in the turn order, 
attempting the exact same action against the same target. If 
that action is no longer possible, it fails without rolling and 
their turn ends. They cannot move or communicate until then. 
This Present Is Not The Future’s Past 
 
The Distortion slips between realities and timelines, itself 
uncertain when or where it belongs. The Distortion and their 
Distorted Palace flicker between interpretations across 
realities, never looking the same for longer than a round. 
 
When rolling a 1 on the risk die in a conflict scene with the 
Distortion, an exorcist gains 1 temporal instability. 
 
Once per hunt, as a reaction, the Distortion reverses time to 
the beginning of the conflict scene. The Distortion and all 
exorcists recover any slashes, stress, sin, KP, psyche bursts, 
or other resources spent that conflict scene except for 
temporal instability. Every exorcist remembers what happened 
and is in the same position they were in when the first round 
began, but gains 1 temporal instability. This domain is not 
recovered as a result of such a reversal.

VECTOR 
The Untethered Mind 
 
The Distortion can rapidly increase velocity of anything it 
touches. As a part of any reaction, the Distortion can fling 
itself up to ½ CAT distance away. It appears to “fly” with 
extreme control, even around corners or through tight openings. 
 
As a complication, the Distortion can imbue velocity into an 
object or living being it touches to send them flying. The 
combined size of the object or being and the range flung must 
equal CAT+2 or less. If used on a living being, either being 
flung or having something flung at them, they take stress as 
per the inflict stress reaction. 
The Unstoppable Force Of Truth 
 
The Distortion has a unique predilection towards projectiles. 
Any projectile that would hit it deals 1 fewer slash. The 
Distortion’s attack range increases to CAT. 
 
As a threat, the Distortion prepares an incredible amount of 
force to unleash on a target in CAT range. If the threat 
resolves, the target takes 3d3 stress. This breaks through 
items and obstacles, but the target may reduce the stress dealt 
by 1 for each reinforced obstacle or bystander in the way. 
Examples include: concrete wall, shipping container, bank door 
I, Of The Neverending Storm 
 
A constant field of force surrounds the Distortion, constantly 
shifting its surroundings. Everything in a short range around 
the Distortion is either pulled towards it or pushed away. At 
the start of each round, the Distortion chooses which direction 
this is, or suppresses the field to become negligible. 
 
Anything up to CAT size or smaller that is unrestrained is 
moved at ½ CAT speed towards it until it stops adjacent to the 
Distortion if pulled or at a short distance away if pushed. 
Rolling a 1-2 on the risk die in the area knocks any equipment 
from the active exorcist’s hands, making any action using them 
that turn hard by default. 
 
Any Vector power that goes against the force requires a PSYCHE 
roll. On a success, it pierces the force but reduces slashes 
dealt by 1. Otherwise, it fails and is entirely nullified.

GATE 
No Distance Can Separate Kindred Souls 
 
The Distortion controls local space, bending and pinching 
reality around it. Anything in a CAT range within the 
Distortion is considered to be within a short range of it. This 
means the Distortion can attack or touch anything within CAT 
range, including taking the Distort the Self reaction. 
 
As part of its reaction, the Distortion can choose to either 
pinch reality to force a target within CAT range to become 
adjacent with it or it can choose to become adjacent to that 
target. It can also do this with objects up to CAT size. 
Cling Not To A Broken Mirror 
 
Reality shifts around the Distortion, never staying in quite 
the right place. Anytime the Distortion moves through a 
location, the admin can choose to make a 1d6 fortune roll. The 
rooms and locations grow (1-3) or shrink (4-6) significantly as 
it passes through. This makes an easy path to follow, but 
navigating it is often hard or risky. 
 
As a threat, the Distortion can completely rearrange an area 
equal to CAT as per the Maze blasphemy. When it does so, it can 
separate any number of exorcists from each other, though there 
is always a possible path connecting them. 
 
These changes revert when the Distortion is executed, spared, 
or demanifests. When a Gate power would alter a room or object 
changed by this ability, the user rolls a 1d6 fortune die. On a 
1-3, the power has no effect on the room or object. 
The Many Arms Which Grasp Infinity 
 
The Distortion can creatively split space in order to duplicate 
its limbs on surfaces within a short range. These can interface 
with technology, pull levers, or otherwise take actions 
alongside the main body. Within a short range, all attempts to 
set-up or do team-work are hard by default. 
 
Once per hunt, after an exorcist takes a risky action, the 
admin can roll 1d3. The Distortion can take that many 
additional reactions immediately, roll risk each time.

SMOTHER 
Swaddled In Nothing 
 
Surrounding the Distortion is an instability of reality. In a 
short range around it, the Distortion can remove one of the 
following properties: 
 
●​ Light 
●​ Sound 
●​ Weight 
●​ Friction 
●​ Electricity 
●​ Internet 
●​ Combustion engines 
●​ Running Water 
●​ Door handles, window latches, zippers, catches 
●​ Open fires 
●​ Smother powers 
 
This may make actions risky or hard, potentially even
