New Major Aspect
The Parrot (Major)
In the wild, Polly wants seeds and nuts. In captivity, all it
gets is a cracker. Unlike most other wild animals,
however, the parrot tends to get a fairly good deal when
domesticated by humanoids simply because it can be
taught to speak, and when these domesticators enjoy the
freedom of the wild and lawless world, like, say, a bunch
of pirates, a parrot’s really only giving up one jungle for
another.
Upon preparing this aspect, the animist selects any
language he does not know, but has heard spoken in the
last week. He gains the ability to speak this language,
though his voice is decidedly that of a squawking parrot
with a heavy piratical accent whenever he speaks a
language
learned
in
this
manner.
He
knows
these
languages for as long as this aspect continues to occupy
the same number of slots. As a swift action, the animist
selects one of the languages he learned in this manner
and slings a barrage of withering nautical insults at a
creature within 30 feet. If that creature can understand
the chosen language, it becomes shaken, following the
rules for demoralization as laid out in the uses of the
Intimidate skill, for 1 round with a DC 10 + 1/2 the
animist’s level + the animist’s Charisma modifier Will
save to negate. This is a language­dependant mind­
affecting ability that relies on verbal components.
Prominence
Each: For each major slot this aspect occupies beyond
the first, the animist learns as additional language that he
has heard spoken in the last week whenever he prepares
it. In addition, for each major slot this aspect occupies
beyond the first, the animist gains a +2 insight bonus to
Profession (sailor) skill checks.
2: If this aspect occupies at least two major slots, the
animist heals for 1d8 + 1/2 his animist level points of
damage whenever he consumes an herbivorous meal,
such as a plate of fruit or a bowl of seeds. This ability can
trigger up to three times per day. In addition, the duration
of
the
animist’s
demoralization
ability
granted
at
Prominence 1 increases by +1 round, to a total of 2
rounds duration.
3: If this aspect occupies at least three major slots, a pair
of multicolored wings bursts out of the animist’s back,
granting a fly speed of 60 feet with good maneuverability.
In addition, creatures demoralized by the ability granted
at Prominence 1 also take 1d8 piercing damage.
4: If this aspect occupies at least four major slots,
creatures
demoralized
by
the
ability
granted
at
Prominence 1 also take bleed damage equal to the
animist’s Charisma modifier. In addition, the animist
knows how to read and write all languages learned
through this aspect.
5: If this aspect occupies five major slots, the animist
gains the ability to perform the Aid Other action as a free
action, but only when the target of his aid is attempting a
skill
check
related
to
the
operation
of
or
general
knowledge of a sailing ship. He may only perform the Aid
Other action once per attempted skill check. Secondly, the
animist heals for an additional +2d8 points of damage
when consuming an herbivorous meal as detailed by his
Prominence 2 ability. Finally, the duration of the animist’s
demoralization ability granted at Prominence 1 increases
by an additional +1 round, to a total of 3 rounds duration.
New Minor Aspects
The Barnacle (Minor)
Creatures in a grapple with the animist take a ­2 penalty
to checks made to break the grapple.
Carrageen (Minor)
Liquids that come into contact with the animist tend to
jellify themselves for a time. As a result, the animist
ignores the effects of difficult terrain if that terrain is
difficult because it is swampy or mucky.
The Seagull (Minor)
As a swift action, the animist performs the steal combat
maneuver. He gains a +2 bonus to his combat maneuver
check if the object he attempts to steal is either shiny or
food; otherwise, he takes a ­2 penalty.
This ability may be used once per day, plus an additional
time per day for every three animist levels he
possesses.
