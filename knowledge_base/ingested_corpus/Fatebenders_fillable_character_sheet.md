Rules cheat sheet
Skill check: d20 + Profession level + Skill level >= DC
Profession or Attribute check: d20 + Profession level >= DC. 
Critical success: rolled a 20. Critical failure: rolled a 1.
      On Critical failure of a Skill check level up that Skill.
Cooperative check: All participants do a check. Rolls don't 
      crit. Keep the best result.
Extended Test: Reach 3 successes before 3 failures to 
      succeed on the Test.
Advantage/ Disadvantage: Roll 1 more die and discard
      one more lowest/ highest roll. 
Drama tokens: Give the GM a token to reroll a check.
Spend an Armour Point to prevent a Wound. DC to repair it = DC to
      Treat the Wound = Result of the Attack check. 
Treating a Wound: If a Treatment check succeeds on the 1st, 2nd or
      3rd attempt, write an hour (H), day (D) or week (W) in place of the
      Wound's DC. Otherwise a month (M). It takes this long to heal.
Get 1 Fatigue from failing an Ability check (A) or an Endurance check
     after a minute without oxygen (O), an hour in cold or heat (H),
     a day without sleep (S) or water (W) or a week without food (F).
Suffer a Wound for every new Fatigue above 2.
      DC to Treat a Wound from Fatigue = 11 + new Fatigue level.
A full night's rest clears all Fatigue.
