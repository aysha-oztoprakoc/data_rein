Introduction
This is a pre-made group of magician characters for Moon Roads. I needed to create some for myself, so they
might as well be generally available.
In the setting of the game, ‘Hand’ is a term for a small group of magicians who work together, though it’s
most often used for a team appointed by a person or organisation with authority to fulfil some task. Most
informal groups don’t feel a need to refer to themselves that way. It carries a sense of having more purpose
binding them together than just hanging out; in context, having the term applied can be a compliment.
So, here is one possible Hand ready to use. They’re envisaged as a permanent group of friendly allies that
can be deputised for whatever task your adventure demands. Three of them are connected to faction groups,
though not closely tied in to them. The fourth, Flit, is unaligned and finding her way — the group has been
given responsibility for looking after her.
Note: it should be pretty easy to alter the characters’ gender, appearance, name, etc if your group wants to.
The ‘what I think about the group’ section is a short-cut, especially to help with one-shots. Attitudes could
certainly evolve from that starting point. If you’re spending more time with the characters, and particularly
if you introduce them to each other in play, you can let players decide their own attitudes.

Compact character format
I’ve done the stats in the compact format rather than as character sheets, so the whole thing will fit on one
page — especially useful if you’re handed the character for a convention one-shot. Players can transfer the
stats to a character sheet if they want. All the abilities are listed to avoid confusion about where they are,
though it does mean a lot of 2s! The magic facets the character has developed are underlined to make them
easier to spot (it doesn’t denote anything more than that) — these are what players should focus on.

Jennifer Clay
Summary. Management wizard of limited patience. Strengths: willpower, smarts and magical lore.
A woman of around 30, wearing a smart trouser suit. Dark brown hair in a ponytail. Usually serious, efficient
— but her patience and respect for authority are limited, and when she snaps she gets things done.
Jennifer was trained through the Convocation (see below), one of the magical orders, and is still its agent —
though the order finds it useful to keep her at arm’s length... away from breakables. She’s one part company
woman and one part maverick troubleshooter; and the nominal leader of this Hand.
The Convocation. The inheritors of the western tradition of scholarly magic, using writing, ritual, symbols
and sometimes extending into alchemy. Outwardly the most powerful order, not least because it has always
had close links with mundane wealth and influence. Prone to internal politics.
What I think about the group
✦Jack — long-time contact and ally; I respect him and he has my back; the rustic thing can get a
bit wearing...
✦Nicky Zhao — he’s a smartarse, he’s trouble, but he’s useful.
✦Flit — not keen on babysitting, but the kid needs the help and she’s trying; I don’t admit that I
like the teaching role; I worry what she could become if she goes wrong.

Jack
Summary. Down-to-earth traveller and folk magician. Strengths: practical skills, physical stuff, advice.
A man of early 50s to early 60s, with a weathered face, short greying beard, and a frame that’s lean but
strong. He wears corduroy trousers, open-necked shirt, neckerchief, and sturdy shoes suitable for work or
walking. Basically he looks like someone from a postcard of a canal boat or farm field.
Down to earth is a good descriptor. He’s quietly good-natured and unflappable. (Unless fisticuffs are
necessary; then he can oblige.) He’s travelled around and seen some stuff. One day he’ll move on from this
group, but for now it seems good. Jack does have a last name, but he tends to use different ones, if he has to.
He’s representing the faction known as the Hexweave (see below), in the informal way of such things.
The Hexweave. A loose alliance of folk magic practitioners, from local witches to wanderers of road and
rail, their magic blends traditional crafts with whatever bits and pieces work.

✦Nicky Zhao — mixing the old ways and the new; he’ll find a deep road or crash.
✦Flit — a strange one; so much second sight is hard; she could use wisdom but you can’t rush it.

Nicky Zhao
Summary. Urban rogue and city druid. Strengths: agility, sneakiness, street culture.
A man in his early 20s, of Chinese ethnicity, with short hair and round green-tinted sunglasses. He wears
casual clothes and jacket.
Nicky is something of a wide boy, a rogue, a trickster, a wanderer of the city and its ways. He is also a druid.
Or at least, he was trained by the Order of the Silver Oak (see below). City druids are a known thing in the
modern era, and the Order can adapt to members who are drawn in that direction. But Nicky was
particularly… irritating, and they found it convenient to let him mostly do his own thing.
His magic isn’t strong, but it is wide-ranging. Mostly he relies on mundane skills and guile, boosted by the
occasional charm. But sometimes, when the city and its stories need a guardian, he shows his teeth.
Order of the Silver Oak. Inheritors of the Celtic bards and druids, focusing on natural magic in service to
the community. Many take on modern style and issues, but some can be stuck in ‘the old ways’.
What I think about the group
✦Jennifer Clay — too stuck in rules, power games and bossing us around; but she’s usually right.
✦Jack — he’s a weird old codger, but he knows the earth, and he’ll help you out.
✦Flit — she’s got all sorts of freaky stuff going on; but she’s also a kid finding herself, I get that.

Flit
Summary. Teenage goth struggling with magical talent. Strengths: intuition, elusive, weirdness.
A late-teens goth girl with black hair, make-up, dark jacket, and messenger bag (containing the friends that
help her do the magic). A bit nervous and over-vigilant; distrustful of people in general, can be sharp.
Flit (birth name Felicity, but she prefers the nickname) is on a journey back to some kind of mental balance.
Her gift manifested without anyone to help her make sense of it. Her high natural Intuition became a curse,
exposing her to every resonance atmosphere and watching creature, in a world where no one else could
relate to her experiences. She developed magic in her own somewhat erratic way. Fortunately she was
found, and has been receiving some mentoring, as well as being assigned to this group for ongoing support.
Externally, she moves between poles of stubborn independence and mute terror. Internally, she’s still rather
dependent. But she’s learning to appreciate the wonders that the magical world offers. (Play note: Flit is
very good at picking up on things with her Intuition, but she often doesn’t understand what it means.)
What I think about the group
✦Jennifer Clay — sometimes stuck up, sometimes scary, but she’s on my side mostly.
✦Jack — he’s like a weird grandpa, and also a big tree.
✦Nicky Zhao — like the class clown, till the inside stuff gets called up. We might be friends?

GM notes
Aldrich Webber. Jennifer’s contact. I see him as 50s–60s, amiable, basically what you’d call ‘good’ but does
have his own schemes operating within the Convocation. He’s genuinely fond of Jennifer, but there are
limits to what he can do for her; especially as she keeps getting herself into trouble. She usually contacts
him by phone.
Mr Fungus. Flit’s contact. She’s able to summon him, given a quiet magic-conducive location and some
time. He appears as a small man (maybe 3 ft tall) with a goat-like head, made of Shadow, with the
impression of varying outfits that fit the situation. His feet are a bit indistinct and he tends to ooze and
spread out, which is why her young self gave him the name (that and a certain book). He speaks kindly to
Flit, and gives titbits of wisdom about the otherworld, magic and even human relationships. He is
unavoidably creepy though. Older Flit has been questioning how wise it is to deal with him, and whether he
has another agenda. She had an instinctive sense (perhaps he encouraged it) that it might be better not to
share his existence with other magicians.
(You should probably share this text privately with the player and let them steer where it goes. Do the other PCs
know about him?)
Nicky’s specialism ‘Manipulate building materials’ is intended to help him use the construction material of
the city, mainly for moving things around or reconfiguring them. Encourage inventive use as long as it
doesn’t get too broad — the Craft section in the book may help.
Jennifer was not consciously based on the comics character Jenny Sparks, but in hindsight I can see the
connection.
Nicky’s appearance is somewhat inspired by Seraph from The Matrix: Revolutions.
Examples and inspiration
Part of the reason for making this is to show you what you can do with Moon Roads characters. To expand on
that, you could easily make variants of these characters, by changing their gifts or other choices.
For example, ideas I had for Nicky included a Specialism in Change (Animal powers) to make him better at
partial transformations; or using Tool to give him a short staff, reflecting the druid background and making
him better at fighting (also props enable fun mannerisms). Flit could have leaned more toward working with
Shadow-stuff. Jennifer could have been more scholarly and liked libraries. Jack could have talked to foxes.
