The official directive of the Bureau is to 
“protect mankind from the threats of the 
Unknown”. In practice, this means 
safeguarding, controlling access too, and 
protecting all information related to the 
paranatural. This includes dimensional 
bleeds, planar anomalies, non-euclidean 
mathematical incursions, monsters, and 
“magic”.  They also conduct extensive 
research in an attempt to better 
understand these phenomena and learn 
to utilize them to aid in the fight against 
the horrors. 
The Bureau maintains jurisdiction on all 
matters paranatural or unknown, 
superseding all other federal, state, or 
local agencies. They can apply whatever 
scope and scale the current Director 
deems necessary to combat these threats, 
including the complete suspension of the 
civil liberties of normal citizens in service 
to “the greater good of humanity”.
The Bureau is a clandestine United States 
government agency that is tasked with 
controlling paranatural phenomena. The 
agency as it is known today was created in 
1905 by the Roosevelt Administration due 
to pressure from several key government 
figures after the truth behind the 
assassination of President McKinley was 
leaked to them.
Due to the nature of their work, the 
Bureau operates outside of official 
oversight. While federal and state 
agencies are aware of the Bureau as an 
agency and the subsequent chain of 
command, their existence and operations 
are kept secret from the public and there 
are very few outside of the agency that are 
truly aware of their operations.

While the Bureau is well equipped and 
well staffed, their reach and resources are 
not unlimited. They will often employ 
contractors to manage “low risk” 
investigative work that would otherwise 
pull agents from more important tasks. 
These contracts rarely offer more than 
cursory details and threat assessments, 
but the pay is moderate and per diem is 
offered if the appropriate paperwork is 
submitted.
The Bureau prefers to give cases to 
reliable individuals, so there’s always 
more work for those who succeed, and 
they will often use the promise of 
potential future full time employment as 
incentive. However, the Bureau keeps a 
close eye on contractors, even well after 
the terms of their contracts are up.
Below are some common contract types:
  › Resonant Artifact containment.
  › Destruction of threats to humanity.
  › Seizing or capturing assets that may 
be useful to the Bureau.
  › Gathering intelligence for potential 
Bureau intervention.
As a government agency, the Bureau has 
been granted extensive federal funding. 
While other agencies and officials are 
vaguely aware of the Bureau’s existence 
and authority, few know of their true 
scope and operation, which allows for 
their funding to be tucked in as forgotten 
line items among national defense bills.
An extensive budget, scope, and access to 
paranatural resources allows for a wide 
range of methodology. Common 
operations include containment, field 
testing, extraction, extermination, 
counter-intelligence, psyops, and the 
application of paranatural abilities. The 
Bureau is also authorized to commandeer 
any local police resources deemed 
necessary for an operation. 
Objects and entities that are deemed a 
significant threat are either terminated or 
captured and held in containment in the 
Monolith for further study. This includes 
an extensive collection of Resonant 
Artifacts (p.x), which are occasionally 
wielded by agents during investigations.
While the Bureau is a U.S. agency, their 
reach extends into a number of other 
nations, often without official approval.
Due to the chain of command, the Bureau 
often works directly with federal and state 
agencies. There have also been rare 
occasions when members of the 
Bloodstone Group (p.x) have been 
contracted for particularly dangerous jobs.
While the Bureau was originally based in 
Washington, D.C. upon their founding, 
their headquarters was officially moved to 
the Monolith (p.x) in Seattle, WA in 1967 
under the tenure of Director Greymoor.
The Bureau also operates field offices in a 
number of major cities throughout the 
country and maintains covert permanent 
and temporary monitoring stations in 
regions of frequent paranatural activity. 
Investigation teams may also setup within 
the offices of local law enforcement 
during coordinated operations.
As an official government agency, the 
Bureau naturally stands opposed to those 
that meddle with the paranatural. This 
including years long investigations into 
the working of The Church of Celestial 
Science (p.x) and Panopticon (p.x), and 
many of the Unaffiliated Hunters of the 
Weird (p.x) have been deemed “Astral 
Terrorists” and have standing warrants for 
their arrest.

act independently of direct oversight.

investigations like any other law officers, 
they are also capable of going deep 
undercover when the situation calls for it. 
This is most commonly done in places 
where frequent paranatural activity occurs 
to monitor the situation and alert Bureau 
management when new situations arise. 
The Sonorous are the Bureau’s 
bloodhounds, able to sense the resonant 
frequencies associated with Resonant 
Artifacts and dimensional anomalies. This 
ability is very rare, so only a handful are 
deployed into the field at one time and 
not all of them are employed by choice.

never sent on assignment alone. They are 
deployed alongside a contingent of Agents 
and Operators, depending on the needs of 
the investigation.

standard gear. However, these individuals

otherworldly forces or from prior 
encounters with the horrors.

with keeping Agents and Sonorous safe, 
both in the field and in the offices.

paranatural forces, they’re kept on a “need 
to know” basis by Bureau management. 
They’re trained to follow orders and not 
ask questions.

has deemed to be “too useful to destroy”. 
These horrors are captured and bound

those that maintain some semblance of 
sentience, and are deployed alongside 
Bureau agents to fight the most dangerous 
threats to humanity.

The Shadow isn’t a simple virus. It doesn’t 
spread by zombie bite or like an airborne 
contagion. Instead, it worms its way into 
the psyche of its hosts through thoughts 
and ideas, carried along by resonant 
frequencies. 
The exact origin point is unknown. Some 
theorize that it’s the result of our 
dimension grinding against another like 
tectonic plates, while others believe it’s 
some form of otherworldly pathogen or a 
non-carbon based parasitic life form. 
What’s clear is that it can spread from 
person to person.
Once the infection takes root, it begins to 
twist the innermost unconscious aspects 
of an individual, manifesting them in an 
outward manner through a physical 
corruption. This is typically in the form of 
a black tar-like substance that oozes out 
of the pours and orifices, but the changes 
are a dark mirror of the host.
Those that are kindhearted or do not have 
part of themselves buried in their 
unconscious are typically safe from the 
spread, as there is little for the Shadow to 
twist and corrupt.
Once the Shadow has taken root within an 
individual, it is only a matter of time until 
they fully merge and are overtaken. To 
stop the spread, the infected must come 
to a understanding and realization of their 
personal unconscious aspects before they 
can be corrupted and warped. However, 
this acknowledgment must be a continual 
process. The Shadow is never truly gone 
and slipping back into your old self will 
allow the corruption to spread once more.
There is no path back to humanity for 
those who have fully lost themselves to 
the Shadow’s influence. Death is the only 
true release.
The Shadow is a corruption. It preys upon 
the unconscious aspects of an individual, 
twisting and warping them until the 
conscious mind is overwhelmed and 
eventually overtaken to become a slave in 
service of unknowable goals.
While the effects vary based on the 
individual, those fully overtaken primarily 
manifest in two forms: Drones and Ghouls.

Often referred to as “haunted”, “cursed”, 
or “possessed” objects, Dissonant 
Artifacts are the byproduct of an 
otherworldly incursion of some kind, as 
they only manifest in locations where the 
dimensional barrier is thin or an extra-
planar incursion has occurred, and will 
often be in the presence of other horrors.
Dissonant Artifacts possess some form of 
simulacrum of life. They are aware of their 
surroundings in some capacity and have 
been known to show signs of emotion and 
intelligence, but little is known of their 
goals beyond a desire to maintain 
existence and create trouble. The 
prevailing theory from Bureau scientists is 
that Dissonant Artifacts are the result of 
lingering thoughts and emotions from a 
person or creature that become 
manifested into resonant energies, though 
testing is inconclusive due to the volatile 
nature of the objects.

A long weekend at a quiet campground on 
the shores of a glass still lake awaits. 
There’s nothing quite like a night under 
the stars away from the congestion of city 
living. But you can’t quite shake the 
feeling that there’s something watching 
you from the trees.
Camp Coldwater is an introductory 
scenario, designed for use with a fresh set 
of Investigators or as a means of kicking 
off a new set of terrors for seasoned 
Investigators.
A wretched creature stalks the forests 
around Camp Coldwater, steadily picking 
off victims. Isolated at a secluded 
campground with intermittent cell service 
and no one else around to help, your 
Investigators will need to use the tools at 
their disposal throughout the campground 
to their advantage if they hope to survive 
the night. 
INTRODUCTION
OVERVIEW
Camp Coldwater is situated amid tall pine 
trees on the shores of Coldwater Lake, just 
a few miles outside of the town of 
Coldwater and down a long gravel road. 
Like the town itself, it used to be a hive of 
tourist activity, booked solid by campers 
for weeks on end during the spring and 
summer, but has since become a shadow 
of its former glory.
Beneath Coldwater is a Mycete (p.x), a 
massive mutated fungal colony that has 
begun spreading its mycelia throughout 
the region. Recent rains have brought its 
fruiting bodies to the surface. These 
bioluminescent mushrooms produce a 
faint blue glow and spread pathogenic 
spores to both animals and people in the 
region, bending them to the demands of 
the central cluster. 
The Mycete’s most recent, and perhaps 
deadliest, creation is the Anthromycin 
(p.x), a wretched hulk of a creature that 
stalks the campground.
Begin the session with the Investigators 
on the gravel road heading towards the 
campground. The sun has already fallen 
behind the trees and it will be dark soon. 
Before letting them loose, ask these 
establishing questions:
  ? You’ve booked a long weekend at the 
campground. What’s your main 
reason for coming to Coldwater?
  ? You’ve crammed yourself into a single 
vehicle. What type of vehicle is it and 
who owns it?
  ? You’re running hours behind your 
original schedule for arriving at the 
campground. What unforeseen event 
caused you to be late?
  ? You’ve agreed to meet other campers 
at the campground. How do you know 
Fred, Deshawn, and Sarah?
