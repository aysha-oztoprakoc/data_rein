Pregen Characters
Red Die System
The following pages are pre-generated characters for use with the Red Die System.
Note that these pregen characters define the technical aspects of the character 
such as Race, Stats, and Abilities. Personal aspects such as Name, Physical 
Description, Persona, and Magic Style are deliberately left out so you may flavor 
each character to your own play style.
