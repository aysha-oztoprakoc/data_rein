All Untouchable squads have 4 primary important mechanical stats. Losses and
Stress are similar to an exorcists injuries. When stress fills, the squad takes losses
to reflect member's of the squad dying. When this happens, roll 1d6 on the Trauma
Response Table. A squad is considered fully killed once they suffer their final loss.
A squad can have a maximum of 6 stress and 6 losses base.
Traits are abilities that either passively apply to exorcists in the scene as long as
they are alive. A response is a special reaction used to enhance an exorcist's
action, usually via violence. 
When doing so, the squad takes the same amount of stress the exorcist suffers, or
none when a 6 is rolled. A squad may also be optionally targeted by an opponent. A
squad can have a collective 6 traits and responses total.

obvious, but in many ways they differ, both in their
use and deployment. Exorcist's function not only as
precise and effective weapons, but also a magnifying
glass to identify and solve the trauma's of a sin's
manifestation. Untouchables are more akin to
beating a problem with several hammers and hoping
the problem goes away. Directed properly or
supporting an exorcist, they are much more effective.

dressed in full armor and carrying weapons. They
have a vehicle made to carry all of them around,
allowing them to tail or follow the exorcists to any
location remaining relatively unnoticed unless
someone is already made aware of them.

force where it may been seen or recorded, roll a fate
die. This may lead to the authorities or other threats
in the hunt area becoming aware much more easily.

so exorcists teams can finish the job, weapons

civilians are useful. A bystander between you and the sin is cover. A crowd that the sin
won't fire into is a shield. A civilian's death, if timed correctly, creates an opening that a

The Stake is your investment. A Snare with nothing on the line has no teeth.
Stakes come in five types.
RESOURCE: Spend something concrete. Mark KP, spend a psyche burst, use a
consumable item, sacrifice a piece of gear, or take stress up front. The resource is
gone whether the Snare fires or not.
POSITION: Give up tactical advantage. Leave cover, separate from allies, enter a
dangerous area, place yourself somewhere bad. The Admin confirms the position
is genuinely worse for the exorcist.
TIME: Spend an additional scene preparing. Tension ticks as normal. The clock is
part of the cost. This STACKS on the time used to prepare the Snare.
INFORMATION: Reveal something true about yourself or your team to the target.
Your real name. Your sinseed location. Your blasphemy's weakness. Your team's
identities. Once given, it cannot be taken back.
VOW: Impose a restriction on yourself for the Snare's duration. You cannot use a
specific blasphemy. You cannot attack the target directly. You cannot speak. You
must protect a specific person. A Vow lasts until the Snare resolves or the scene
ends, whichever is later. If the Snare fails, the Vow still holds.
Vows count as two Stakes because they impose ongoing cost.
You may stack multiple Stakes. Each additional Stake increases the Jaw's power.

NU appears to be able to essentially grant an “anti-blasphemy” known as
VOID.

blasphemy is similar to the virtue FAITH but on a more minor level. Its
theorized that a suitable number of void users could truly accomplish
Cain’s mission though its near impossible to find them. The very nature
of VOID makes its user identical to a graceless human, thus tracking and
sourcing them is an intensive endeavor that even Cain can’t truly afford.

When running Nubodies always remember that their
priority is rescuing exorcists. Unlike Under/Heaven,
Nubodies will never directly harm exorcists or allow them to
come to harm when possible. They will attempt to convince
exorcists to follow or forcibly move them dependent on the
situation and will always let them go if they choose to
return to Cain. 
It should be noted that a good number of Nubodies have
experience in either psychology or experience with
maladaptive personalities. This is added to the fact that
majority of these Nubodies are direct friends and family of
the exorcist or individuals sent on their behalf.
Nubodies granted psychic powers by NU will always come
in 2 fashions: Void and Harpy. 
Harpy users are the most common as they appear in active
execution zones. They are prone to direct confrontation in
attempts to extract exorcists as quickly as possible. 
Void users are far less common often appearing briefly and
in moments of high distress or death. They often appear
either delivering something to an exorcist or most
natoriously right before an exorcist flees Cain.
